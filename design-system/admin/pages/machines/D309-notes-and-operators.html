      What it is

      Notes about one machine and the people who run it. Opens with ?m=ZP-TF-2310.

      What you can do

      • Add, edit or pin a note.
      • Hide a note from the customer.
      • Add operators with their training date.

      What happens next

      Notes show in the machine history (D314) and, unless hidden, in the app.

      Edge cases and spec

      • Hidden notes show to Zixom and engineers only.
      • Notes from the app cannot be hidden from their author.
      • Untrained operators get a Book training action.
