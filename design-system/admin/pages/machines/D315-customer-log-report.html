Customer log report

Printable log for one machine or one customer. Replaces the paper register page.

Large report, running in the background. 6 machines, 41 visits. You can leave this page; a notification arrives when it is ready. Exports

What it is

The customer log: machine, warranty, AMC, every visit, parts and notes. Opens with ?m=ZP-TF-2310.

What you can do

  • Choose one machine or one customer.
  • Download as PDF or Excel.
  • Leave hidden notes out for a copy the customer sees.

What happens next

Large reports land in Exports (D1520).

Edge cases and spec

  • Large reports run in the background (EC-A34).
  • PDF fails: Could not create the PDF with Retry (EC-A30).
  • Hindi and special characters print correctly (EC-A08).
