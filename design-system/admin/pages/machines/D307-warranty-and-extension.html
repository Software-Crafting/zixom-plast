      Open warranty policy

      What it is

      Warranty of one machine: dates, type, terms and every change. Opens with ?m=ZP-TF-2204.

      What you can do

      • Extend with a reason and a document.
      • Change the type or terms.
      • Void with a reason.

      What happens next

      Back to Machine detail (D302); claims on the machine (D502).

      Edge cases and spec

      • 12 months from the start date for all models; reminders at 60, 30 and 7 days.
      • Changing dates with claims: old decisions stay (EC-A38).
      • A claim for an ended warranty needs an extension first.
