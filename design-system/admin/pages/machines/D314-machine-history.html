What it is

Everything that happened to one machine, newest first. Opens with ?m=ZP-TF-2310.

What you can do

  • Filter by type and date, search the text.
  • Open any ticket, claim or warranty entry.

What happens next

Ticket detail (D403), Claim detail (D502), Warranty (D307), Customer log report (D315).

Edge cases and spec

  • Long timelines load in pages of 50.
  • Print hides navigation and repeats headers (EC-A58).
