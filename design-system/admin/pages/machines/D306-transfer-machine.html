What it is

Move a machine to another customer in three steps. Opens with ?m=ZP-PC-1805.

What you can do

  • Choose the new customer and site.
  • Decide what happens to each open ticket and the AMC.
  • Set the date and reason, then confirm with your password.

What happens next

Back to Machine detail (D302); the change lands in the history (D314).

Edge cases and spec

  • Open ticket or AMC: listed with Move, Keep with old customer or Cancel (EC-A37).
  • History always stays with the machine.
  • Password needed; both companies are notified.
