What it is

Modal over the machine to set its status. Opens with ?m=ZP-TF-2310.

What you can do

  • Choose Running, Idle, Repair or Decommissioned.
  • Give a reason.
  • Decommissioned asks for a date.

What happens next

Back to Machine detail (D302); the change lands in the history (D314).

Edge cases and spec

  • Decommissioned hides service actions in the app and makes the machine read only.
  • The serial stays reserved for ever.
  • Machines reported in the app are added on D302 or D305 first.
