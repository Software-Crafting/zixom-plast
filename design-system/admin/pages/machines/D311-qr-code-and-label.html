What it is

The QR label for one machine. Opens with ?m=ZP-TF-2310.

What you can do

  • Print one label or a sheet of 12.
  • Download the QR as PNG.
  • Copy the app link.

What happens next

A customer scans it in the app (C205) and lands on the machine (C202).

Edge cases and spec

  • The QR encodes the serial as a zixomconnect:// link.
  • PDF fails: Could not create the PDF with Retry (EC-A30).
  • Prototype QR is a look-alike pattern.
