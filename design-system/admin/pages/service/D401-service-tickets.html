What it is

Every service request from the app, phone or email. Table or board.

What you can do

  • Search, filter, use saved views, choose columns.
  • Click a row for the quick view; click the number for the full ticket.
  • Select rows to assign or export in bulk.
  • Board: drag a card to an allowed next status.

What happens next

Ticket detail (D403), Assign engineer (D404), New ticket (D402), Bulk result (D1809).

Edge cases and spec

  • Unassigned machine down tickets are pinned at the top in red.
  • 25 rows by default (25, 50, 100). Export over 5,000 rows runs in the background.
  • Bulk with some failures shows done and failed counts (EC-A64).
