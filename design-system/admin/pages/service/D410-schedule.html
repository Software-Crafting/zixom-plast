What it is

Engineers as rows, time as columns. Day, week and month.

What you can do

  • Click a block for the ticket drawer.
  • Drag an unassigned ticket onto an engineer (opens Assign).
  • Drag a block to another time (Reschedule) or engineer (Assign).
  • Click empty time to create a ticket.

What happens next

Assign engineer (D404), Reschedule (D405), New ticket (D402), Ticket detail (D403).

Edge cases and spec

  • Overlapping blocks stack with a warning (EC-A47).
  • Dropping on leave or a weekly off is refused (EC-A46).
  • Red line is now. Refreshes every 30 seconds.
