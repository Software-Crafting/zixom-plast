Reschedule

Customer used 2 of 2 reschedules. You can still move it with a reason.

Pick a free slot. Starred slots are the customer's choice.

What it is

Drawer over the ticket to move the visit. Opens with ?t=; default TKT-2026-0123.

What you can do

  • See the current window and the engineer's free slots.
  • Propose a new window with a reason.
  • Approve or decline the customer's request (Request state).

What happens next

Customer and engineer are told. Back to Ticket detail (D403).

Edge cases and spec

  • Customer limit: 2 reschedules, not within 4 hours of the window.
  • After travel started the request waits for you (EC-A49, try TKT-2026-0125).
