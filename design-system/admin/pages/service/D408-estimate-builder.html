    What it is

    Estimate for a chargeable job or extra work. Opens with ?t=; default TKT-2026-0122.

    What you can do

    • Add labour, parts, travel or other lines; change quantity and rate.
    • Set a discount, validity and an advance.
    • Save a draft or send it to the customer.

    What happens next

    The customer approves or rejects in the app (C509). Sent estimates are read only; Revise makes a new version.

    Edge cases and spec

    • Warranty lines are zero and locked (try TKT-2026-0126).
    • Out of stock part shows the lead time.
    • Saved lines keep their rate when the rate list changes.
    • Offline: Save is disabled.
