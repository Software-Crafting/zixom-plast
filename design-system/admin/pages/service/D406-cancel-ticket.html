What it is

Modal over the ticket to cancel it. Opens with ?t=; default TKT-2026-0123.

What you can do

  • Pick a reason and add a note.
  • Cancel the ticket, or keep it.

What happens next

Customer and engineer are told. The ticket shows Cancelled; Duplicate as new stays available.

Edge cases and spec

  • If a payment exists the refund is shown (Paid state).
  • Status moved in the app meanwhile: "This ticket changed. Reloaded." (EC-A14).
