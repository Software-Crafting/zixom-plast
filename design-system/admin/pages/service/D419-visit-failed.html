What next

What it is

Drawer over a ticket whose visit failed. Opens with ?t=; default TKT-2026-0130.

What you can do

  • See the reason, photo, wait time and call attempts.
  • Reschedule, charge the visit fee or close.

What happens next

Reschedule (D405). The fee becomes an invoice line (D902). Close ends the ticket.

Edge cases and spec

  • The fee applies only where the policy says so (Confirm).
  • Customer wait is 20 minutes, then Visit failed with a photo.
