What it is

Drawer over the ticket. In progress shows Hold; On hold shows Resume. Default TKT-2026-0126.

What you can do

  • Hold: reason, expected resume date and a note.
  • Resume: keep the engineer or pick a new window.

What happens next

Customer and engineer are told. Resume with a new window opens Reschedule (D405).

Edge cases and spec

  • Reasons follow the engineer's list (E208).
  • Resume: open TKT-2026-0127.
