What it is

Drawer over the ticket to pick an engineer and a visit window. Opens with ?t=; default TKT-2026-0133.

What you can do

  • Pick a suggested engineer; hover the score for why.
  • Pick a date and slot; the customer's slots are marked.
  • Assign and send offer, or Assign directly with a reason.

What happens next

Offer: the engineer has 30 minutes to accept (E203). Direct: the engineer is told. Back to Ticket detail (D403).

Edge cases and spec

  • Leave blocks without a reason (EC-A46). Overlap warns (EC-A47).
  • Pincode not serviceable (EC-A48). Another Admin first (EC-A13).
  • Phone not synced in 24 hours: may not receive the offer.
  • On the way: reassign blocked (try TKT-2026-0125).
