What it is

Requests from customers to reopen a closed ticket (C515), within 7 days.

What you can do

  • Open a request to see the reason and photos.
  • Accept and reopen (same engineer by default), decline with a reason, or convert to a new ticket.

What happens next

Accepted tickets show Reopened and go back to Assigned (D403). Convert opens New ticket (D402).

Edge cases and spec

  • Past 7 days needs an override reason (TKT-2026-0129).
  • The customer is told either way.
