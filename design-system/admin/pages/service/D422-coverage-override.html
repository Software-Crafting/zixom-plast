What it is

Modal over the ticket to change Warranty, AMC or Chargeable. Opens with ?t=; default TKT-2026-0122.

What you can do

  • See why the current coverage was set.
  • Pick the new coverage and see the change in charges.
  • Save with a reason.

What happens next

Written to history. The customer sees the new coverage and any change in charges; open estimates are revised (D408).

Edge cases and spec

  • AMC covers labour and travel, not parts.
  • A reason is required.
