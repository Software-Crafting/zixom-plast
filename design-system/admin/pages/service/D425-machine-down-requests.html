What it is

Machine down requests from the app (C1501, C1502), pinned until handled.

What you can do

  • Call the customer and log the result.
  • Acknowledge to stop the repeat alerts.
  • Assign now, directly if needed.

What happens next

Assign engineer (D404); the engineer is told at once on a direct assignment.

Edge cases and spec

  • Not acknowledged in 15 minutes: email and SMS to the emergency number (Confirm).
  • After hours flag (TKT-2026-0126, 07:45).
  • Sound is optional in Settings (D1605).
