What it is

One quotation with its PDF preview, revisions and who opened it. Opens with ?q=QT-2026-0014-R2; try 0010 to 0013.

What you can do

  • Only the actions the status allows show (section 11).
  • Mark accepted records who accepted and how, including by phone.
  • Mark rejected asks for a reason. Convert an accepted one to an order.

What happens next

Revise (D1202), Order detail (D1211), Lead detail (D1103), Customer detail (D202).

Edge cases and spec

  • Revisions keep the number with R2, R3; older versions stay read only.
  • Expired quotations can be revised.
  • PDF failure shows Retry; the quotation stays saved (EC-A30).
