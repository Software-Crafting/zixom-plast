What it is

The machine and product master quotations are built from: specification, options, base rate, GST and brochure.

What you can do

  • Search, filter and switch views by kind.
  • Click a row for details; Add or Edit in a drawer.
  • Archive products you no longer sell.

What happens next

Products appear in the picker of Create quotation (D1202). Spare parts live in the parts catalogue (D801).

Edge cases and spec

  • Used by quotations: archive hides it from new ones only.
  • GST 0, 5, 12, 18 or 28 percent; HSN or SAC 4 to 8 digits.
  • Website store is Phase 2.
