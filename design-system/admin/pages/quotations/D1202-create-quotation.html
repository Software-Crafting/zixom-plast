      What it is

      Quotation builder for a lead or customer. Opens the draft QT-2026-0010; ?q=QT-2026-0014-R2&revise=1 opens a revision.

      What you can do

      • Pick products with options, set quantity, rate and discount; totals update as you type.
      • Choose payment terms, validity and terms templates.
      • Save draft, preview the PDF, send by email or save as a new revision.

      What happens next

      Quotation detail (D1203). The lead moves to Quotation sent (D1103).

      Edge cases and spec

      • Autosave every 10 seconds; versions are kept.
      • Discount above 5% needs a reason (try 8 on a line).
      • PDF failure keeps the draft and offers Retry (EC-A30).
