What it is

Every machine sale quotation for leads and customers, with value, status and validity.

What you can do

  • Search, filter, use saved views, choose columns.
  • Click a row for the quick view; click the number for the full quotation.
  • Select rows to download PDFs, export or mark expired.

What happens next

New quotation (D1202), Quotation detail (D1203), Products (D1204), Terms (D1205), Export (D1521).

Edge cases and spec

  • Statuses: Draft, Sent, Viewed, Accepted, Rejected, Expired, Revised (section 11).
  • Revisions keep the number with R2, R3.
  • Valid until within 7 days shows amber. Export over 5,000 rows runs in the background (EC-A34).
