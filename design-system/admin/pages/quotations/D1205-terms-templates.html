What it is

Reusable terms and conditions and payment terms for quotations.

What you can do

  • Click a template to edit it in a drawer.
  • Add a template and mark one default per type.
  • Payment milestones must add up to 100%.

What happens next

Templates are picked in Create quotation (D1202). Machine sale invoices follow the payment milestones (D902).

Edge cases and spec

  • Default machine sale payment is 30, 40, 30 and editable (section 12 rules).
  • Editing a template does not change sent quotations.
  • Percent fields accept 0 to 100 with 2 decimals.
