What it is

Milestones of a turnkey project with dates, owners, notes and photos. Opens with ?o=PRJ-2026-001.

What you can do

  • Add or edit a milestone in a drawer; click a row to edit.
  • Mark a milestone done.
  • Share the progress with the customer by email.

What happens next

Order detail (D1211). The last milestone leads to Dispatch and installation (D1213).

Edge cases and spec

  • Statuses: Not started, In progress, Done, Delayed (section 12).
  • Delayed needs a new date and a reason; the reason shows in the list.
  • Mark done records today's date.
