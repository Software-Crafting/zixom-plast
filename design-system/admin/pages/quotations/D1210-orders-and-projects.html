What it is

Machine orders and turnkey projects from confirmation to commissioning, with payments received.

What you can do

  • Search, filter and use saved views.
  • Click a row for the quick view; click the number for the full order.
  • Select rows to export or send a stage update.

What happens next

Order detail (D1211), Project milestones (D1212), Dispatch and installation (D1213).

Edge cases and spec

  • Stages: Confirmed, Design, Manufacturing, Testing, Dispatched, Installed, Commissioned (section 11).
  • Late orders are red and pinned on top.
  • Payments follow 30, 40, 30 by default.
