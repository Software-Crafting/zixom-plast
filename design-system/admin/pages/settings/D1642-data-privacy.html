What it is

Personal data requests and how long each kind of record is kept.

What you can do

  • Export one person's data as a ZIP, with a reason and your password.
  • Open deletion requests (D209).
  • Change retention periods.

What happens next

The ZIP appears in Exports (D1520). Every export and change is logged.

Edge cases and spec

  • Deletion with open tickets, unpaid invoices or active AMC is blocked until cleared (EC-A53).
  • Invoices are kept for tax law even after deletion; the person is anonymised.
  • Activity log 3 years (Confirm), integration and delivery logs 90 days.
