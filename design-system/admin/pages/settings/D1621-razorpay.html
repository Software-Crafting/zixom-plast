Rotate Razorpay keys?

Paste the new keys from Razorpay. The old keys stop working here when you save. Enter your password to continue.

What it is

Razorpay keys, webhook, mode and methods.

What you can do

  • Switch Test or Live, choose methods, save.
  • Copy the webhook address.
  • Test the connection, rotate keys with your password and a reason.

What happens next

New payments use the new settings. Each webhook is written to the integration log (D1641).

Edge cases and spec

  • Secrets shown masked, never revealed.
  • Bad signature: rejected 401, logged, alert after 3 in 10 minutes (EC-A20).
  • Duplicate webhook ignored by payment id (EC-A19).
