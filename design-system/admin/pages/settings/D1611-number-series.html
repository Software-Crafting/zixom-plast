What it is

Every record number the system issues: tickets, claims, invoices, receipts and more.

What you can do

  • Search the series.
  • Open a row to raise its next number, with a reason.

What happens next

The next record of that type takes the new number. The change is in the activity log.

Edge cases and spec

  • Next number can only go up: try a lower number.
  • Year series restart at 0001 on 1 April.
  • Invoice numbers are assigned by the server with no gaps (EC-A43). Format Q6.
