Reset to the default text?

What it is

Editable text on the service report, invoice, estimate, quotation, AMC contract, statement and label.

What you can do

  • Pick a template, edit its text blocks; the preview updates as you type.
  • Open the PDF with sample data, see versions, reset to default.
  • Publish a new version.

What happens next

New documents use the published version. Issued documents keep the version they were made with.

Edge cases and spec

  • Versions kept; preview uses sample data (Shree Packaging).
  • PDF generation fails: 'Could not create the PDF' with Retry (EC-A30).
  • Business details come from Business profile (D1604).
