Refund message

Shown in the app when a refund starts.

Up to 300 characters. Used in App C1408 and on the receipt.

What it is

Payment methods, pending checks, offline proof, refund message, invoice reminders and credit terms.

What you can do

  • Turn methods on or off.
  • Change check times, reminder days and default credit terms.
  • Edit the refund message.

What happens next

Saved toast. The app and reminder job use the new rules within 15 minutes.

Edge cases and spec

  • Gateway keys are in Integrations (D1621), not here.
  • Pending check: every 5 s for 2 min, then on each open; Admin job 24 h (section 14).
  • Credit terms default 15 days (Confirm); a customer can have its own.
