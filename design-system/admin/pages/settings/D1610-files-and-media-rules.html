Files and media

Size and count limits. Apply to the app and the dashboard.

Allowed file types

Every upload is virus scanned. Other types are refused with a reason (EC-A29).

What it is

How many photos, how long a video and how large a file can be, everywhere.

What you can do

  • Change limits and allowed types.
  • Reset a limit, open its history.

What happens next

Saved toast. The app enforces the new limits within 15 minutes; files already uploaded stay.

Edge cases and spec

  • Defaults: 10 photos, 1 video of 60 s and 50 MB, 10 MB per photo or PDF, voice note 2 min (section 14).
  • Too large or failed upload: per file error with Retry (EC-A29).
