Sign in and security

Codes, locks, sessions and devices for app users and Admins.

Rules can be made stricter. A value below the safe minimum is refused.

What it is

How people sign in: code length and expiry, resend, locks, sessions, devices, biometric lock.

What you can do

  • Tighten a rule for app users or Admins.
  • Open the history of a row.

What happens next

Saved toast. New sign ins use the rule; signed in sessions keep theirs until they end.

Edge cases and spec

  • Cannot be loosened below safe minimums: try typing 8 wrong tries or a 12 minute code.
  • Admin session 8 hours with 30 minute idle timeout (Confirm).
  • Locked Admins can be unlocked from Users (EC-A54).
