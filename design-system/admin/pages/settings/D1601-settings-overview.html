What it is

The home of Administration. One card per settings page with when it last changed.

What you can do

  • Search settings by name or by a word they contain.
  • Filter by group.
  • Open any settings page; the left menu stays on every settings page.

What happens next

Each card opens its page, for example Service rules (D1605) or App control (D1630).

Edge cases and spec

  • Every setting shows its value, default, where it is used and its history (section 14).
  • The app picks up a change within 15 minutes (EC-A62).
  • Every change is in the activity log (D1640).
