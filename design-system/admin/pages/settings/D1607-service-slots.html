What happens next

Saved toast. The slot picker (C1510) and Reschedule (D405) use the new slots within 15 minutes.

Edge cases and spec

  • Defaults: 9 AM to 12 PM, 12 to 3 PM, 3 to 6 PM; minimum notice 4 hours (section 14).
  • Visits already booked keep their window.
  • Overlapping slots are refused.
