What it is

Grouped settings. Each shows its value, default, where it is used and a history link.

What you can do

  • Change a value; Save turns on.
  • Reset one setting to its default.
  • Open its change history.
  • Changing a response target asks for a reason.

What happens next

Saved toast. The app picks up the change within 15 minutes (Pending state).

Edge cases and spec

  • Values that break rules are refused, for example an accept window under 5 minutes.
  • Open tickets keep their rules; new events use the new ones.
  • Two Admins change the same setting: EC-A12 (Conflict state).
  • Values from section 14; targets marked Confirm.
