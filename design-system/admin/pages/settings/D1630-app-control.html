What it is

App versions, force update, maintenance, unsupported phones, feature switches and demo data.

What you can do

  • Set latest and minimum versions; turn force update on.
  • Turn maintenance on (asks first) or schedule it.
  • Switch features on or off. Preview S01 and S02.

What happens next

Saved toast. The app reads these at launch and within 15 minutes. Maintenance on shows a red bar on the dashboard.

Edge cases and spec

  • Minimum higher than latest is refused: try 2.5.0.
  • Force update to a version not in the store: warning.
  • Maintenance during a payment: status checks finish, new actions blocked; engineers mid visit keep working offline (EC-A63).
