What it is

One card per outside service with its status, last event and masked keys. Tally is first.

What you can do

  • Tally: import invoices, export receipts, see the automatic sync plan.
  • Test a connection.
  • Open a service's setup (Razorpay D1621, IndiaMART D1111, Email and SMS D1622, Backups D1643).
  • Rotate keys with your password and a reason.

What happens next

Every call is written to the integration log (D1641). Failures alert on the dashboard.

Edge cases and spec

  • Test mode banner on every page while Razorpay is in test (Test mode state).
  • SMS provider down: alert, switch provider (Partial error state, EC-A28).
  • Without maps, suggestions use pincode and region only.
  • Open with the client: TallyPrime or Tally ERP 9 (decides automatic sync); who uploads the Tally export each day.
