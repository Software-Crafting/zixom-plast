What it is

Email sender details, SMS provider and the DLT templates, with delivery status.

What you can do

  • Change sender name, from and reply to.
  • Switch SMS provider.
  • Send a test email or SMS.

What happens next

Messages use the new sender at once. Every send is in the delivery log (D1320).

Edge cases and spec

  • Bounced email is marked and the person shows Email not working (EC-A27).
  • SMS provider down: alert and switch provider (EC-A28).
  • A template not yet registered cannot be sent.
