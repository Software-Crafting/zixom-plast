      Versions

      What it is

      The checklist an engineer fills in a visit report, one per machine type, with guidance per item.

      What you can do

      • Pick a machine type.
      • Add, edit, reorder (drag the handle) or remove items in the draft.
      • Publish the draft as a new version.

      What happens next

      The app uses the new version for new reports within 15 minutes (E410).

      Edge cases and spec

      • Reports store the version used, so old reports stay readable.
      • Type with no template: the app shows the general checklist with a notice.
