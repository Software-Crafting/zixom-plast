What it is

Seven lists: decline, hold, cancel, reject, dispute, visit failed, return.

What you can do

  • Switch lists with the tabs, search.
  • Add a reason, edit, reorder or disable one.

What happens next

The app shows the new list within 15 minutes. Reports and filters use the same wording.

Edge cases and spec

  • A reason in use cannot be deleted, only disabled; past records keep it.
  • Other is always last and asks for a note.
