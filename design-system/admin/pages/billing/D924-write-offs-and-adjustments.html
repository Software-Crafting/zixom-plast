What it is

Small leftover balances, such as bank charges the customer's bank deducted, and the write off history.

What you can do

  • Write off a balance under the limit with a reason and your password.
  • Change the limit in Payment rules (D1609).

What happens next

The invoice becomes Paid; the write off shows in reports (D1501) and the invoice history.

Edge cases and spec

  • Only balances under the limit (₹500) can be written off here.
  • Money action: password and reason (section 14).
  • Never deletes the invoice.
