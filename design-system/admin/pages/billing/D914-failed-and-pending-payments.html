What it is

Online payments that failed or are still processing after 30 minutes.

What you can do

  • Re-check one, several or all with Razorpay.
  • Contact the customer by phone or in the app.

What happens next

The customer sees C806 (failed) or C807 (pending) in the app. Payment detail (D911).

Edge cases and spec

  • Pending payments are re-checked automatically for 24 hours.
  • Failed: debit returns in 5 to 7 working days.
  • A late webhook moves a row out of this list (EC-A18).
