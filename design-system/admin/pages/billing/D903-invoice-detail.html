What it is

One GST invoice imported from Tally: lines, payments, credit notes, reminders and the PDF. Opens with ?inv=INV-2026-0981; try 0985, 0986, 0987, 0983.

What you can do

  • Only the actions the status allows show (section 11).
  • Record an offline payment, send a reminder, download the PDF.
  • GST lines are read only: Correct in Tally and import again.

What happens next

Import (D1510), Verify proof (D912), Refund (D922), Follow up (D925), Suspend AMC (D610).

Edge cases and spec

  • Credit notes and cancellations are made in Tally and come back with the next import (EC-A42).
  • Side card shows the import time and Tally voucher.
  • PDF failure shows Retry; the invoice stays saved (EC-A30).
  • Open dispute and amount mismatch show banners (EC-A21).
