Field

What it is

Requests from the app to fix a GSTIN, company name or billing address on an invoice.

What you can do

  • Row click shows the old and new details side by side.
  • Correct and reissue: credit note plus a new invoice (password and reason).
  • Decline with a reason.

What happens next

The customer is notified in the app. The new invoice opens in Invoice detail (D903); the note in Credit notes (D904).

Edge cases and spec

  • Amounts, items and dates cannot be changed this way (C1604).
  • After the month's GST return is filed, decline is the usual answer.
  • Invoice numbers are never reused (EC-A43).
