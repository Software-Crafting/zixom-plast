What it is

The reminder schedule and every reminder sent or planned.

What you can do

  • Send a reminder now or skip one.

What happens next

Customers see reminders as notifications in the app.

Edge cases and spec

  • Paused while an online payment is processing or a proof waits.
  • No email on record: push only (EC-A61).
  • Quiet hours 9 PM to 7 AM.
