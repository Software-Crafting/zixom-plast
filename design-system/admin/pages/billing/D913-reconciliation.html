    What it is

    What Razorpay paid into the bank against the payments it captured, for a date range.

    What you can do

    • Change the range; export to Excel or CSV.
    • Mark a matched settlement reconciled, one or in bulk.
    • Open any payment from the list.

    What happens next

    Reconciled settlements are locked. Unmatched payments link to Payment detail (D911).

    Edge cases and spec

    • Unmatched payments are listed at the top.
    • Fees include 18 percent GST on the fee.
    • Amount mismatch payments stay unmatched until reviewed (EC-A21).
