What it is

Every payment: Razorpay online payments, bank transfer, UPI and cheque proofs.

What you can do

  • Search, filter by method and date, saved views.
  • Row click for the quick view; the payment id opens the detail.
  • Payments menu: failed and pending, reconciliation, receipts, refunds.

What happens next

Payment detail (D911), Offline verification (D912), Refund (D922).

Edge cases and spec

  • Amount mismatch is flagged; the invoice stays Processing (EC-A21).
  • Failed payments show the gateway reason.
  • Duplicate and late webhooks never create a second row (EC-A18, EC-A19).
