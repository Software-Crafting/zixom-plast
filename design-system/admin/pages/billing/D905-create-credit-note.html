What it is

Credit part or all of an invoice. Opens with ?inv=; default INV-2026-0980, disputed in DSP-2026-0009.

What you can do

  • Pick lines and quantities, or switch to a plain amount.
  • Choose to reduce the balance or refund.
  • Issue with your password and a reason.

What happens next

The note shows in Credit notes (D904) and the app (C1603). Refund choice opens Create refund (D922).

Edge cases and spec

  • Cannot exceed the invoice (Over limit state).
  • Paid invoices are corrected only with a credit note (EC-A42).
  • Money action: password and reason (section 14).
