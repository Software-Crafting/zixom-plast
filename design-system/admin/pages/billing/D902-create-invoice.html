What the customer sees in the app

What it is

GST invoice builder. Opened from the ticket TKT-2026-0114 with lines from the report.

What you can do

  • Change the company or place of supply; tax switches between IGST and CGST plus SGST.
  • Edit lines: quantity, rate, discount and GST rate recalculate the totals.
  • Save draft (autosaved), preview the PDF, issue.

What happens next

Issue assigns the next number, makes the PDF and notifies the customer. Then Invoice detail (D903).

Edge cases and spec

  • Supplier Haryana (06); Gujarat and Maharashtra get IGST. Pick Haryana to see CGST plus SGST.
  • Number only at issue, in sequence (EC-A43). PDF failure keeps the invoice (EC-A30).
  • Over ₹1,00,000 asks for the password. Deactivated company blocked.
