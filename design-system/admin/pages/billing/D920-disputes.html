What it is

Charges customers disputed in the app (C1601), one line per dispute.

What you can do

  • Search, filter, saved views.
  • Click a row to open the dispute and decide.

What happens next

Dispute detail (D921): accept with a credit note, partly accept, reject or ask for more.

Edge cases and spec

  • Decide within 3 working days; older turns red.
  • One open dispute per line (the app links to it).
  • Same numbers as the app (DSP-2026-0009, 0007, 0005, 0004).
