What it is

Account statement per customer for a date range.

What you can do

  • Pick the customer and a preset or custom range.
  • Download the PDF or send it by email.

What happens next

Large ranges run in the background and land in Exports (D1520).

Edge cases and spec

  • Failed, pending and unverified payments are left out.
  • Credit notes reduce the balance; refunds add back.
  • Empty range: Nothing in this range.
