What it is

One disputed charge with the line highlighted. Opens with ?d=DSP-2026-0007; try 0009, 0004, 0005.

What you can do

  • Read the reason and evidence; message the customer.
  • Accept (credit note), partly accept, reject with a reason, or ask for more.
  • Credit notes ask for your password and a reason.

What happens next

The customer is notified in the app (C1602). Accepting creates a credit note (D904); a paid invoice can be refunded (D922).

Edge cases and spec

  • Decide within 3 working days.
  • Accepted and rejected disputes are read only.
  • Refund after a dispute is linked to it and its credit note.
