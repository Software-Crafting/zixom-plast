What it is

Every customer company with its people, machines, open tickets and dues.

What you can do

  • Search, filter, use saved views, choose columns.
  • Click a row for the quick view; the code opens the full record.
  • Select rows to export or send statements.

What happens next

Customer detail (D202), Add customer (D203), Edit company (D204), Import (D1510), People (D216).

Edge cases and spec

  • Companies exist only because Admin created or approved them.
  • ZP-C-0003 has the same GSTIN as ZP-C-0001: flagged, merge in D212.
  • Deactivated companies are read only and grey.
  • Long names truncate with a tooltip (EC-A07).
