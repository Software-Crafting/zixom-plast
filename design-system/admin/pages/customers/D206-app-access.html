What it is

Who can use the customer app. There is no sign up: Zixom adds people and sends access by SMS; they sign in with mobile and OTP. ?tab=requests and ?tab=unknown open the other tabs.

What you can do

  • People: filter by access, resend the SMS, disable or enable access.
  • Requests from the app: Add person opens D205 filled in; Decline with a reason.
  • Unknown numbers: add the number as a person or ignore it.

What happens next

Add person (D205), Customer detail (D202), Devices (D208). Requests and unknown numbers also sit in the work queue (D102).

Edge cases and spec

  • Invited turns to Never signed in after 7 days without a sign in.
  • Only the primary contact can request a new user; the requester is told the outcome in the app.
  • An unknown number sees "Not registered, contact Zixom" in the app.
  • Disabled people are signed out at once (A16).
