What it is

Cases where a mobile number or email is on two accounts, or on an engineer and a customer.

What you can do

  • Compare both accounts side by side with their last activity.
  • Keep one, move the number to the other, ask the person to use another number, or merge two customer people.

What happens next

The case moves to Resolved. The person can sign in again (A20 clears).

Edge cases and spec

  • Each resolution asks for a reason and notifies the person.
  • A number cannot be both customer and engineer: merge is not offered (section 2 rules).
  • Raised by EC-A35 and by imports with duplicates (EC-A32).
