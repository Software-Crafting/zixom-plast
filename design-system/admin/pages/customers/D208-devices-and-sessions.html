What it is

Every phone signed in to the app: model, system, app version, last active and push state.

What you can do

  • Click a row to see the person's devices.
  • Remove a device, sign out everywhere, reset the device limit once, send a test push.

What happens next

A removed device is signed out the next time the app opens (A15). At the limit the app shows A23.

Edge cases and spec

  • Limit: customers 2 devices, engineers 1 (section 14).
  • Reset device limit allows one extra sign in, once.
  • Expired push token: the push fails and shows in the delivery log (EC-A26).
