What it is

Every invitation to the app, sent by a primary contact in the app or by Admin here.

What you can do

  • Resend, revoke or copy the invitation link.
  • Click a row for details; the name opens the person.

What happens next

The person accepts in the app (A18) and becomes Active in People (D216).

Edge cases and spec

  • Invitations are valid 7 days. Expired and revoked rows are grey.
  • Resend starts a new 7 day link; the old one stops working.
  • Up to 25 people per company (section 14).
