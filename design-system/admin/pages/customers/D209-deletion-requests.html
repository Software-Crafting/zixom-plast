What it is

Requests to delete an account, made in the app (A24).

What you can do

  • Click a row to see blockers and the deletion date.
  • Approve, reject with a reason, extend the wait, or cancel on the person's request.

What happens next

On approval personal data is erased; tickets, invoices and machine history are kept anonymised (A25 in the app).

Edge cases and spec

  • Approve is disabled while blockers exist: open tickets, unpaid invoices, active AMC, pending refunds (EC-A53).
  • 30 day wait from the request (flow F15).
  • The person can cancel in the app; the row moves to Cancelled.
