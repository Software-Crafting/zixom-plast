What it is

Create a customer company with its first site and primary contact. ?v=VER-2026-0020 opens it filled from a verification request.

What you can do

  • Fill the form; GSTIN is checked as you type.
  • Save, Save and add machine, or Save and invite the primary contact.

What happens next

Customer detail (D202), Add machine (D303), Invitations (D206). From a request: the person is approved and linked.

Edge cases and spec

  • GSTIN: 15 character pattern with a check digit.
  • Duplicate GSTIN or mobile warns with a link to the existing company (Duplicate state).
  • Invalid pincode; required fields (Errors state).
  • Offline keeps the data (EC-A01); Save disables on first click (EC-A09).
