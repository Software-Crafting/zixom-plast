    What it is

    The one place a customer is created. Zixom adds the company, sites, people and machines; there is no sign up in the app.

    What you can do

    • Fill the five sections; add more sites, people and machines.
    • Mark one person as the primary contact.
    • Send app access by SMS (on by default), then see who got it (Done state).

    What happens next

    Customer detail (D202), App access (D206). People sign in with mobile and OTP; machines show in their app with warranty.

    Edge cases and spec

    • GSTIN: 15 character pattern with a check digit.
    • Duplicate GSTIN, mobile or serial warns with a link (Duplicate state). An engineer's mobile is blocked.
    • Invalid pincode; required fields (Errors state).
    • Warranty end = installation date + 12 months.
    • Offline keeps the data (EC-A01); Save disables on first click (EC-A09).
