    What it is

    Join a duplicate company into the real one. Opens with ?c=shree-dup (ZP-C-0003, same GSTIN as ZP-C-0001).

    What you can do

    • Choose the company to keep.
    • Pick name, GSTIN and address for the result.
    • Merge with your password.

    What happens next

    People, sites, machines, tickets, invoices and AMC move. People see one company in the app. Back to Customer detail (D202).

    Edge cases and spec

    • Irreversible warning; password required.
    • The same person in both companies keeps one link (Rakesh M and Rakesh Mehta: resolve in D207 first).
    • Merged company is archived and redirects to the kept one.
