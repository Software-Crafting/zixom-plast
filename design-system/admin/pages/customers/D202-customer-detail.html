What it is

One customer company: people, sites, machines, service and money. Opens with ?c=shree or ?c=nova.

What you can do

  • Edit the company, add a person or a site.
  • Create a ticket, invoice or quotation for this company.
  • Change the primary contact; merge or deactivate from More.

What happens next

Edit (D204), Person (D205), Merge (D212), Deactivate (D213), Primary contact (D214), Ticket (D402), Invoice (D902).

Edge cases and spec

  • Each tab loads on first open with a skeleton and its own error (Tab error).
  • Deactivated: grey banner and read only tabs; only Reactivate.
  • Same GSTIN on two companies shows a merge banner (?c=shree-dup).
