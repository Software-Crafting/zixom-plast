What it is

Add a person to a company or edit one. Only Zixom adds people. ?p=pooja edits, ?c=nova adds to Nova Cups, ?request=harish fills from a request in the app, ?mobile=9890122874 from an unknown number.

What you can do

  • Edit details, companies, sites and the primary flag.
  • Send app access by SMS (on by default); resend while they have not signed in.
  • Disable, enable or remove from the company.

What happens next

App access (D206), Devices (D208), Change primary (D214). The person signs in with mobile and OTP; from a request, the primary contact is told. Disabled people see A16 in the app.

Edge cases and spec

  • Mobile used by an engineer is blocked (Blocked state, D207).
  • Mobile used at another company adds this company to that person.
  • The primary contact cannot be disabled until another is chosen.
  • Up to 25 people per company, 5 companies per person.
