What it is

Edit a company's details, status, credit terms and internal notes. Opens with ?c=shree.

What you can do

  • Change any field and save.
  • A new GSTIN asks for a reason, kept in history.
  • Deactivate goes to D213.

What happens next

Back to Customer detail (D202) with a Saved toast.

Edge cases and spec

  • Another Admin saved first: Stale data banner, second save refused (EC-A12).
  • Deactivated companies open read only (Read only).
  • Leaving with unsaved changes asks first (D1808).
