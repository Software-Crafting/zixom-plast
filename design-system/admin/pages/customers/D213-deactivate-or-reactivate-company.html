What it is

Deactivate a company with a reason, or reactivate it. ?c=shree deactivates; ?c=ganga or ?c=kaveri reactivates.

What you can do

  • Decide what happens to each open ticket first.
  • Pick a reason and confirm.

What happens next

People of a deactivated company see A16 and cannot sign in. The company turns read only (D202).

Edge cases and spec

  • Open tickets are listed and need a decision before Deactivate is enabled.
  • Active AMC and dues are shown; dues stay collectable.
  • Reactivate restores sign in for active people.
