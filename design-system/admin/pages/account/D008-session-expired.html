What it is

Shown when the session ends. In the product it sits over the page as a modal; every signed in page opens it in the Session expired state.

What you can do

  • Enter the password and sign in again.
  • Warning: 2 minutes before the end, Stay signed in keeps the session.

What happens next

Returns to the same page with the unsaved data restored (here TKT-2026-0126, D403).

Edge cases and spec

  • Session lasts 8 hours with a 30 minute idle timeout (Confirm, section 14).
  • Unsaved data is restored; if the record changed, EC-A12 applies (EC-A04).
  • Sign out in one tab signs out all tabs in 5 seconds (EC-A05).
