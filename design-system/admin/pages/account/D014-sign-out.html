What it is

Confirms sign out. Opened from the profile menu at the bottom of the sidebar.

What you can do

  • Sign out of this browser, or tick to sign out on all others too.
  • Cancel returns to the page.

What happens next

Signed out, then Sign in (D001).

Edge cases and spec

  • Unsaved changes are listed before signing out (Unsaved).
  • Other tabs sign out within 5 seconds (EC-A05).
  • Other browsers are listed in Active sessions (D011).
