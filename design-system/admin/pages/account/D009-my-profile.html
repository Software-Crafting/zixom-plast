What it is

The signed in Admin's own profile. Opened from the name at the bottom of the sidebar.

What you can do

  • Edit name, title and photo, then Save.
  • Change email or mobile with a code sent to the new one.
  • Open password, sessions, activity and alerts.

What happens next

Change password (D010), Active sessions (D011), My activity (D012), Notification settings (D013), Sign out (D014).

Edge cases and spec

  • New email is verified by a code; the old email gets a notice (EC-A56).
  • Email or mobile used by another account (Duplicate).
  • Time zone is fixed to IST (EC-A57).
