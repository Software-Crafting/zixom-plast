    Open queue

      What it is

      A four step tour over the dashboard on the first sign in: sidebar, search, work queue, Create.

      What you can do

      • Next moves through the steps; Skip closes the tour at any time.
      • Tick Don't show again to stop it.

      What happens next

      The dashboard (D101). The tour can be reopened from the help icon (D1644).

      Edge cases and spec

      • Shows once per Admin.
      • On a phone the sidebar and work queue steps point at the menu button.
      • Done shows the page after the tour.
