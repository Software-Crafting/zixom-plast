What it is

Your own actions, taken from the activity log (D1640).

What you can do

  • Switch views by type, search after 2 characters, filter by date and type.
  • Click a row to open the record.
  • Export to Excel or CSV.

What happens next

Rows open the ticket (D403), invoice (D903), customer (D202), setting or account page.

Edge cases and spec

  • Skeleton, empty and no results states.
  • Times show in IST (EC-A57).
  • The log cannot be edited; the full log for all Admins is D1640.
