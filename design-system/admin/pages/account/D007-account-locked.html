What it is

Shown after 5 wrong passwords or codes. The account is locked for 15 minutes.

What you can do

  • Wait for the countdown.
  • Back to sign in, or reset the password.

What happens next

When the countdown ends the page returns to Sign in (D001). Ending starts at 0:05.

Edge cases and spec

  • 5 tries, 15 minute lock (section 14, EC-A54).
  • Another Admin can unlock from Users (D1602).
  • Flow F13.
