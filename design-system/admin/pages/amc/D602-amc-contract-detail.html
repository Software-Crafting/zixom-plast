What it is

One AMC contract. Opens with ?amc=AMC-2026-0010; try 0011 to 0017.

What you can do

  • Only the actions allowed for the status show (section 11).
  • Tabs: overview, machines, visits, invoices, documents, history.
  • Download the contract PDF, send a renewal reminder.

What happens next

Create or renew (D603), Visit planner (D607), Suspend or reactivate (D610), Invoice (D903).

Edge cases and spec

  • Contract PDF comes from the template (D1612); a failed PDF shows Retry (EC-A30).
  • A machine removed from a contract keeps its history.
  • Expired: Renew only within the 7 day grace period.
  • An action from an old page is refused (EC-A14).
