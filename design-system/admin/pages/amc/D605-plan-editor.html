What it is

Edit one AMC plan. ?plan=Essential, ?plan=new for a blank plan.

What you can do

  • Name, description, visits, response time, parts discount.
  • Lines included and not included.
  • Price by machine series and term; terms text.
  • Save draft or Publish.

What happens next

Back to Plans (D604). Published changes show in the app (C403) on next open.

Edge cases and spec

  • The preview updates as you type.
  • A plan in use: changes apply to new contracts and renewals only (In use state).
  • App picks up the change within 15 minutes (EC-A62).
