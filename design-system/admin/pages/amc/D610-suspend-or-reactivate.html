Reason
In the app
    Cancel

    What it is

    Suspend or reactivate an AMC. ?amc=AMC-2026-0013 reactivates; ?amc=AMC-2026-0010 suspends.

    What you can do

    • Pick a reason: non payment, customer request, other.
    • See what changes in the app, then confirm.

    What happens next

    Back to the contract (D602) with the new status; the customer is notified.

    Edge cases and spec

    • Suspended AMC shows a banner and Pay now in the app.
    • Visits are on hold and tickets are not covered while suspended.
    • Only Active, Expiring (suspend) and Suspended (reactivate) allow this (section 11).
