What it is

Planned AMC visits per contract and machine, as a calendar or a list.

What you can do

  • Schedule a visit: creates a Preventive maintenance ticket.
  • Reschedule, or mark missed with a reason.
  • Click a visit to open it (D608).

What happens next

Visit detail (D608), the ticket (D403), reschedule requests from the app (D612).

Edge cases and spec

  • Due in 7 days with no engineer is amber.
  • Visits of a suspended AMC are on hold.
  • Reminders go 7 days and 1 day before the visit.
