What it is

Wizard to create, renew or edit an AMC. ?renew=AMC-2026-0011 fills it from the old contract.

What you can do

  • Pick customer, site, machines, plan, term and start.
  • Use the plan price or a custom price with a reason.
  • Create as Pending payment (invoice and link) or Active (offline payment received).

What happens next

The contract opens (D602). Pending payment sends invoice and link; visits go to the planner (D607).

Edge cases and spec

  • Overlap with another AMC is blocked (EC-A39, Overlap state).
  • Custom price needs a reason.
  • Active without payment asks for the reference and your password (section 14).
