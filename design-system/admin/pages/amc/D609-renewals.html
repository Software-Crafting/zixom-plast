What it is

Contracts ending in 60 days, in the 7 day grace period, or lapsed.

What you can do

  • Send a reminder now, create the renewal (D603).
  • Call the customer, mark will not renew.

What happens next

Renewal opens Create contract (D603) filled from the old contract. Will not renew goes to D611.

Edge cases and spec

  • Reminders 60, 30, 7 days before and on the end date.
  • Grace ends 7 days after expiry; then a renewal is a new contract.
  • Ending in 7 days or in grace is red.
