What it is

AMC requests from Buy AMC in the app that need a price.

What you can do

  • Send a quotation or an invoice.
  • Convert to a contract (D603).
  • Decline with a reason.

What happens next

Customer accepts or pays in the app; the contract becomes Pending payment, then Active (D602).

Edge cases and spec

  • Waiting over 2 working days is red.
  • Price changed in the plan: the customer sees the new total.
  • Machine taken by another AMC meanwhile: convert is blocked (EC-A39).
