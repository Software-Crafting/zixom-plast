    Leave

    What it is

    Asks before leaving a form with unsaved changes. Shown over Edit company (D204).

    What you can do

    • Stay keeps the form and the changes.
    • Leave drops the changes and opens the link.

    What happens next

    Leave opens the page clicked (here the customer, D202). Form shows the page without the dialog.

    Edge cases and spec

    • In app links show this dialog; browser close and refresh show the browser's own warning (EC-A06).
    • Drafts restore where autosave exists.
