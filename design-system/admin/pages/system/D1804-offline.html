What it is

Shown when the network is lost and the page cannot load.

What you can do

  • Try again now.
  • Wait: the page retries when the network returns.

What happens next

Back online reloads the page (Back online).

Edge cases and spec

  • Forms keep their data; nothing is saved twice (EC-A01).
  • On other pages offline is a banner over the page.
