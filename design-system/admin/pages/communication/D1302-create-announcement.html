What it is

Write a notice for the app and see it in the phone as you type. Open an existing one with ?n=ANN-2026-001 (sent), 002 (scheduled) or 003 (draft).

What you can do

  • Title, text, image, link, audience with a live count, pin, show dates.
  • Push, email, both or in app only; send now or schedule.
  • Save draft, send a test to yourself, withdraw after sending.

What happens next

Back to Announcements (D1301) with reach; push results in the delivery log (D1320).

Edge cases and spec

  • Scheduled in the past is blocked; Send now is offered (EC-A65, state Past).
  • Audience of 0: send is disabled (state Zero).
  • More than 3 pinned: warning (state Pinned).
  • Draft autosaves. Withdrawn is removed on next refresh; delivered push cannot be recalled.
