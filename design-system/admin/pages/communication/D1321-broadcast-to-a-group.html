What it is

A quick message to a customer, a company, an engineer or everyone.

What you can do

  • Choose who gets it; the count updates.
  • Use a template or free text, pick push, email or SMS.
  • Send.

What happens next

It lands in the app's Notifications; results are in the delivery log (D1320).

Edge cases and spec

  • Customers get no push 9 PM to 7 AM (state Quiet); engineers always.
  • Person with no email gets SMS instead (EC-A61).
  • For a notice that stays in the app use an announcement (D1302).
