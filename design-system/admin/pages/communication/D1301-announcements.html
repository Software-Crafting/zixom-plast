What it is

Every notice sent to the app: draft, scheduled, sent, withdrawn or expired.

What you can do

What happens next

Create or open (D1302), delivery of each push (D1320).

Edge cases and spec

  • Sent items are read only except Withdraw (section 11).
  • Reach counts delivered pushes and opens; failures are in D1320.
  • More than 3 pinned shows a warning in D1302.
