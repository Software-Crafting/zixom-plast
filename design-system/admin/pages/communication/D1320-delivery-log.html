What it is

Every push, email and SMS sent in the last 90 days with its result. Open with ?ref=ANN-2026-001 to see one announcement.

What you can do

  • Search, filter, use the views for failures.
  • Row click shows details and the error; resend one or many.
  • Export to Excel or CSV.

What happens next

Fix the person's contact in Customer detail (D202); change text in Templates (D1310).

Edge cases and spec

  • Push with an invalid token is Failed; email goes as backup (EC-A26).
  • Bounced email flags the person card (EC-A27).
  • No email on file: falls back to SMS (EC-A61).
  • Retention 90 days (section 14).
