Reset to the default text?

Your push, email and SMS text for this event is replaced by Zixom's default. Publish to use it.

What it is

Text of one notification for push, email and SMS. Opens with ?e=assigned; also try ?e=otp, ?e=offer, ?e=amc-renewal.

What you can do

  • Edit the text, insert variables, see it as the customer or the engineer gets it.
  • Save a draft, publish, reset to default, send a test.

What happens next

Back to templates (D1310); sends show in the delivery log (D1320).

Edge cases and spec

  • Push title 50, body 140 characters; over the limit blocks Publish (state Limit).
  • Critical templates can be edited, not disabled.
  • An unknown variable shows as an error in the preview.
