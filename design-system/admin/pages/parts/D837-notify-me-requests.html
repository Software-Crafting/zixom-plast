What it is

Who tapped Notify me on an out of stock part in the app.

What you can do

  • See who waits for each part and since when.
  • Notify all once stock is added, if it was not sent automatically.
  • Open the part or the reorder list.

What happens next

A receipt (D812) that lifts stock above zero sends the notice by push and email.

Edge cases and spec

  • Sent automatically when stock moves above zero.
  • Notify all is off while the part is still out of stock.
  • A discontinued part moves its requests to the replacement.
