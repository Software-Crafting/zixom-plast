    What it is

    A bulk price change with a preview and an effective date.

    What you can do

    • Pick parts by category, choose percent or amount, rounding and date.
    • Apply now or schedule; cancel a scheduled change.

    What happens next

    The catalogue (D801) and the app show the new prices from the date.

    Edge cases and spec

    • Carts with the old price show an update in the app (EC-44 in the app).
    • Quotes already sent keep their price until they expire.
