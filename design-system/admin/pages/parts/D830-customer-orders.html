What it is

Spare part orders and quote requests customers place in the app.

What you can do

  • Search, filter, use saved views, choose columns.
  • Click a row for the quick view; click the number for the order.
  • Select rows to export or remind about payment.

What happens next

Order detail (D831), Quote builder (D832), Pick and pack (D833), Shipment (D834), Returns (D835).

Edge cases and spec

  • Statuses and actions follow section 11.
  • Price on request orders show no total until quoted.
  • Exports over 5,000 rows run in the background.
