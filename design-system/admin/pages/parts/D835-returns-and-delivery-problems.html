Type

What it is

Returns and delivery problems customers report in the app after delivery.

What you can do

  • Click a row to see the photos and decide.
  • Approve return with pickup, reject, replace, refund or ask for a photo.

What happens next

Refund (D922); a replacement is a new no charge order (D831); the returned part is received into stock (D812).

Edge cases and spec

  • Reports are allowed for 7 days after delivery.
  • A discontinued part is replaced by its replacement (ZP-SP-005 to 006).
  • Refunds ask for the password and a reason.
