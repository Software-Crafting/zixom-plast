Ledger

    What it is

    A stock adjustment with a reason. ?p=ZP-SP-016 picks the part.

    What you can do

    • Enter the counted quantity; the difference updates.
    • Choose a reason and add a note.
    • Save.

    What happens next

    The ledger (D811) gets an Adjustment entry; the stock chip updates.

    Edge cases and spec

    • Over 10 units or 10 percent is flagged as large (Large state).
    • Reason is required.
    • Going below minimum adds the part to the reorder list (D815).
