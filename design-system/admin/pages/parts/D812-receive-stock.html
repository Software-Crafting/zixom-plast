Summary

After saving

    What it is

    A goods receipt: supplier, document number, date, lines with cost, attachments.

    What you can do

    • Add or remove lines; quantities and totals update.
    • Attach the supplier invoice or challan.
    • Save the receipt.

    What happens next

    Stock rises, the ledger (D811) gets receipts, reorder alerts clear, Notify me goes out for parts back above zero (D837).

    Edge cases and spec

    • A document number already received warns (Duplicate state).
    • Purchase orders to suppliers are out of scope.
    • One stock location.
