What it is

Pick list and packing checklist for a paid order. ?o=ORD-2026-0208; the To pack state shows it before packing.

What you can do

  • Tick lines and checklist items; Mark packed unlocks when all are done.
  • Enter boxes and weight; print the pick list.

What happens next

The order becomes Packed; then add the shipment (D834).

Edge cases and spec

  • Stock is issued when packed (ledger D811).
  • After packing the order can no longer be cancelled; a problem becomes a return (D835).
