What it is

Stock by part in the one stock location, with what is reserved and free.

What you can do

  • Receive, issue, adjust; export.
  • Click a row for the quick view with recent movements.
  • Open the ledger or the reorder list.

What happens next

Receive (D812), Issue (D813), Adjust (D814), Ledger (D811), Reorder list (D815).

Edge cases and spec

  • Low rows have an amber bar, out rows a red bar.
  • Reserved comes from approved orders and requests; issued when packed or dispatched.
  • Value of stock uses the cost price.
