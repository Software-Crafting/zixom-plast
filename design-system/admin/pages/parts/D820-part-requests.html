Approve selected requests

Requests with enough stock are approved and reserved. Out of stock lines are skipped and listed.

What it is

Parts asked for by engineers from a job, and customer quote requests from the app.

What you can do

  • Search, filter, use saved views.
  • Click a row for the quick view; click the number for the full request.
  • Select rows to approve in bulk.

What happens next

Request detail (D821); customer quote requests open the order (D831, D832).

Edge cases and spec

  • Needed for this visit turns red when over 2 hours old.
  • Bulk approve skips out of stock lines (EC-A64).
  • Warranty tickets get parts at zero.
