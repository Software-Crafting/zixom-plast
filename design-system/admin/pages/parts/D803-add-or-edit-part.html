What it is

The form for a new part, or an existing one with ?p=ZP-SP-001.

What you can do

  • Set number, name, category, specs, images, price or On request, GST, stock levels, supplier and models.
  • Save, or save and add another.

What happens next

Part detail (D802). The app shows the part at once when visible.

Edge cases and spec

  • Duplicate number is blocked (Duplicate state).
  • Empty price means On request.
  • Leaving with changes asks first (D1808).
