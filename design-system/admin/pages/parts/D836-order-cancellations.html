Cancel is only allowed before an order is packed. Paid orders are refunded.

No cancelled orders

Orders cancelled in the app or by Admin appear here with the refund state.

Close this cancellation?

Only when the refund shows as processed. It moves to Closed.

What it is

Cancelled orders and the state of their refund.

What you can do

  • Refund a paid order that has no refund yet (D922).
  • Track a refund already started.
  • Close once the refund is processed.

What happens next

Refunds (D923); the customer sees the refund timeline in the app (C708).

Edge cases and spec

  • Cancel is only allowed before packed; after that it is a return (D835).
  • Refund cannot be more than the amount paid (EC-A40).
  • Refunds ask for the password and a reason.
