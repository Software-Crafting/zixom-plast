What it is

Discontinue a part and name its replacement. Opens over the part page; ?p= picks the part.

What you can do

  • Choose the replacement part.
  • Sell the remaining stock first or stop at once.
  • Keep the part to close without changes.

What happens next

The part shows Discontinued (D802); the app shows No longer made with the replacement (C703).

Edge cases and spec

  • Open carts are updated to the replacement.
  • Notify me requests move to the replacement.
  • Open orders keep the old part.
