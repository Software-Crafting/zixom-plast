What it is

All stock movements with the balance after each. ?p=ZP-SP-001 shows one part.

What you can do

  • Filter by type, part and date; search references.
  • Open the ticket, order or request behind an entry.
  • Export to Excel or CSV.

What happens next

A wrong entry is corrected with an adjustment (D814).

Edge cases and spec

  • Entries cannot be edited or deleted.
  • Stock issued for an order is written when it is packed.
  • Exports over 5,000 rows run in the background (D1520).
