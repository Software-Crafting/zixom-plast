What it is

A quote for a price on request item or a new order. ?o=ORD-2026-0205; ?o=ORD-2026-0206 revises a sent quote.

What you can do

  • Set rate, discount, delivery charge, validity and a note; totals update.
  • Add parts, save a draft, send.

What happens next

The customer approves in the app (C708), then pays. The order moves to Quoted, then Approved (D831).

Edge cases and spec

  • Quotes expire after the validity date (Expired state).
  • A revision replaces the old quote; the customer sees the new one.
  • Drafts autosave every 10 seconds.
