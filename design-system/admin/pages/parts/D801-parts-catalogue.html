What it is

Every spare part the app sells, with price, GST and stock.

What you can do

  • Search, filter, use saved views, choose columns.
  • Click a row for the quick view; click the number for the part.
  • Select rows to change price, show or hide in the app.

What happens next

Part detail (D802), Add part (D803), Import (D807), Price changes (D806).

Edge cases and spec

  • Stock chip: In stock above minimum, Low at or below minimum, Out at zero.
  • No price shows On request; marked parts show Discontinued.
  • Hidden parts stay on open orders.
