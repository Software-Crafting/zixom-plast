What it is

Take parts out of stock. ?r=SPR-2026-0030 fills it from a part request.

What you can do

  • Choose ticket, order or engineer, then the reference.
  • Add lines; available and after update.
  • Issue.

What happens next

The ledger (D811) gets one issue per line; the request (D821) moves to Issued.

Edge cases and spec

  • Negative stock is blocked unless Allow negative stock is ticked with a reason; the part is flagged (EC-A44, Negative state).
  • Orders are normally issued by Pick and pack (D833).
