What it is

Courier and tracking for a packed order. ?o=ORD-2026-0208 (packed) or ?o=ORD-2026-0209 to update tracking.

What you can do

  • Record courier, tracking number and link, dates, boxes and weight.
  • Ship part of the order (Partial state).
  • Save, or send the details to the customer.

What happens next

The order becomes Dispatched (D831); the customer sees tracking in the app (C708).

Edge cases and spec

  • Courier booking is out of scope.
  • No tracking link: the app shows the number only.
  • Partial shipments are separate; the order is Dispatched when all are sent.
