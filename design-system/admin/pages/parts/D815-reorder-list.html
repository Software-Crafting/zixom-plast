What it is

Parts at or below their minimum, with a suggested quantity and supplier.

What you can do

  • Mark ordered with an expected date.
  • Dismiss for 7 days.
  • Export to send to suppliers.

What happens next

A receipt (D812) above the minimum clears the part from the list.

Edge cases and spec

  • The count shows as the Low stock parts card on the dashboard (D101).
  • Purchase orders to suppliers are out of scope; record the order only.
