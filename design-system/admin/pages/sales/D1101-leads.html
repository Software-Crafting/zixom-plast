What it is

Every enquiry from IndiaMART, phone, email, referral and the app. Table or board by stage.

What you can do

  • Search, filter, saved views, columns.
  • Row click opens a quick view; the number opens the lead.
  • Bulk assign owner or change stage; import and export.
  • Board: drag a card to the next stage.

What happens next

Lead detail (D1103), New lead (D1102), Follow ups (D1105), Create quotation (D1202).

Edge cases and spec

  • Overdue follow ups are red.
  • Won needs a quotation, order or note; Lost needs a reason.
  • Bulk with failures shows done and failed (EC-A64).
