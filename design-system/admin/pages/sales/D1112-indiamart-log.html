What it is
The raw record of every webhook request, for checking and fixing.

  
What you can do
  • View the payload, reprocess a failed request, download.
  • Switch to Failed and rejected.

  
What happens next
Reprocessed items show in IndiaMART inbox (D1110). Key problems are fixed in Setup (D1111).

  
Edge cases and spec
  • Failed and rejected requests are highlighted.
  • 401 with an old key shows a hint to paste the new key.
  • Webhook answers within 2 seconds; processing runs after. Kept 90 days.
