What it is

Pairs that look like the same person or company.

What you can do

  • Compare side by side.
  • Merge into the lead that stays, or mark Not a duplicate.

What happens next

The kept lead opens in Lead detail (D1103); a customer match can open Customer detail (D202).

Edge cases and spec

  • Merge keeps both histories; the other lead is archived (section 18 copy).
  • Same mobile within 30 days is linked automatically; older matches come here.
  • Same IndiaMART query id is never a duplicate here: it is ignored (EC-A22).
