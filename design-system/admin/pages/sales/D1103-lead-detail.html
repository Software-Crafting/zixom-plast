What it is

One enquiry from first contact to won or lost. Opens with ?l=LD-2026-0049; try 0047 (overdue), 0041 (existing customer), 0043 (won), 0042 (lost).

What you can do

  • Log a call, add a note, set a follow up.
  • Change stage, create a quotation, mark lost.
  • Convert to customer or create a ticket.

What happens next

Create quotation (D1202), Add customer prefilled (D203), New ticket (D402), Duplicates (D1106).

Edge cases and spec

  • Won needs a quotation, order or note; Lost needs a reason.
  • Existing customer shows a banner; you can make a ticket instead (EC-A24).
  • IndiaMART type shows as a chip; missed calls are flagged.
