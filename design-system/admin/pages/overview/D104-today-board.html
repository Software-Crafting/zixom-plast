What it is

Today's visits by stage, from offer to done. Refreshes every 15 seconds.

What you can do

  • Click a card for the ticket drawer.
  • Drag a card to another column to reassign (opens D404).
  • Filter by region or engineer; search by ticket or customer.

What happens next

Ticket detail (D403), Assign engineer (D404), Schedule (D410), Engineer map (D411).

Edge cases and spec

  • Empty columns say None.
  • An engineer whose app is silent shows 'Last seen {time}' (Engineer offline state).
  • Unassigned machine down is flagged above the board.
