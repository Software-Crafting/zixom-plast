    What it is

    Every pending decision in one list, grouped by type with counts.

    What you can do

    • Pick a type on the left; sort by due, age or priority.
    • Click a row to act in the drawer; Next item moves on.
    • Snooze for 1 hour or until tomorrow; mark follow ups as done.

    What happens next

    The record pages: Ticket (D403), Assign (D404), App access (D206), Add person (D205), Unlisted machine reports (D305), Offline payment (D912), Report review (D413), Part request (D821).

    Edge cases and spec

    • Machine down is always on top; missed targets are red and sorted next.
    • Live: new rows flash; an item handled elsewhere goes with 'Handled by {name}'.
    • User requests and machine reports come from the primary contact in the app; answer within 1 working day (D1605).
    • Sign ins from numbers Zixom has not added show as User requests.
