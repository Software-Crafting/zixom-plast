    Complaint needs a ticket or order and goes to the service manager.

    What it is

    The seven question categories and their order in the app form.

    What you can do

    • Rename a category.
    • Move it up or down, then Save.

    What happens next

    The app picks up the new names and order on next open (EC-A62).

    Edge cases and spec

    • Complaint cannot be renamed or removed: it drives the service manager route.
    • Renaming keeps the category on old questions.
