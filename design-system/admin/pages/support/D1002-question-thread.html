What it is

One customer question: facts, the thread and the customer's recent records. Try ?q=QRY-2026-0032 (closed) or ?q=QRY-2026-0033 (complaint).

What you can do

  • Reply, insert a canned reply, attach a file.
  • Turn on Internal note: the customer never sees it.
  • Mark answered, close, reopen, convert to a ticket or lead.

What happens next

The customer gets a push and sees the reply in C603. Convert opens New ticket (D402) or New lead (D1102).

Edge cases and spec

  • Live: a new customer message appears and flashes.
  • Failed send keeps the text with Retry.
  • Complaint: an owner is required before a reply.
  • Deleted customer shows 'Deleted user'; a file that fails the virus scan is blocked.
