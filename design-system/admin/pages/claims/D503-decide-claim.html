What it is

Drawer over the claim to approve, reject or ask for information. ?c=WCL-2026-0041, &choice=info or reject.

What you can do

  • Approve: coverage, ticket severity and priority, engineer, message.
  • Request information: up to 5 questions, deadline (7 days).
  • Reject: reason, explanation of 20 to 500 characters, offer a paid visit.

What happens next

Approve creates a Warranty ticket (D403) linked to the claim; the customer sees the decision in C308.

Edge cases and spec

  • Warranty ended before the fault: Approve is hidden (?c=WCL-2026-0039).
  • Claim cancelled meanwhile: drawer closes with a message.
  • Customer replies while deciding: reply shows first and must be confirmed.
  • Decision locked after 24 hours except by appeal (D505).
