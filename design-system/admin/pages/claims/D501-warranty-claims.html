What it is

Every warranty claim from the app, phone or email.

What you can do

  • Use the saved views: To review, Information requested, Appeals.
  • Click a row for the quick view; click the number for the claim.
  • Decide from the quick view. Export selected rows.

What happens next

Claim detail (D502), Decide claim (D503), Information requests (D504), Appeals (D505).

Edge cases and spec

  • Review target 2 working days; claims past it are red.
  • Past the information deadline is red; auto reject 7 days after the request.
  • Rejected with an open appeal reads Appeal here and Rejected in the app.
