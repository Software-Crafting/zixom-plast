What it is

One warranty claim. Opens with ?c=WCL-2026-0041; try 0039 (out of warranty), 0040 (duplicate), 0042, 0043, 0044.

What you can do

  • Only the actions the status allows show (section 11).
  • Check the evidence, message the customer, see the decision and linked ticket.
  • Decide opens the decision drawer.

What happens next

Decide claim (D503), Ticket (D403), Appeals (D505), Warranty (D307).

Edge cases and spec

  • Machine out of warranty: red banner, Reject suggested.
  • Same fault on the same machine within 30 days: flagged.
  • Opening a Submitted claim marks it Under review.
  • A decision cannot change after 24 hours except by appeal.
