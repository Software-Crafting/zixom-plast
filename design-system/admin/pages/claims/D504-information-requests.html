What it is

Claims waiting for the customer to answer your questions.

What you can do

  • Send a reminder, extend the deadline, or reject for no response.
  • Select rows to remind or extend in bulk.

What happens next

The customer replies in the app (C308); the claim goes back to Under review (D502).

Edge cases and spec

  • Past the deadline is red.
  • No reply 7 days after the request: rejected automatically with a message (Settings).
  • Partial bulk failures show done and failed counts (EC-A64).
