What it is

Appeals customers send against rejected claims (C310). ?c=WCL-2026-0044 opens one.

What you can do

  • Read the original decision, the customer's reason and new evidence.
  • Uphold with a reason, overturn and approve, or ask for more.

What happens next

Overturn creates the ticket (D403); the claim shows the outcome (D502, C308).

Edge cases and spec

  • Answer within 3 working days; late appeals are red.
  • Customers can appeal within 7 days of the rejection.
  • Upheld appeals are final.
