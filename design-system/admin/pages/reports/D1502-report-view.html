IST

What it is

One report: chart, then a table with totals. Opens with ?r=tickets; try ?r=payments or ?r=quotations.

What you can do

  • Filter by date, region, customer, engineer and series.
  • Tickets: group by status, severity, engineer, region or age.
  • Click a row to open the matching records.
  • Export to Excel or PDF, save the view, schedule an email.

What happens next

Drill down to the list (D401, D901, D1201, D1210 and others), Scheduled reports (D1503), Exports (D1520).

Edge cases and spec

  • Large reports run in the background and land in Exports (EC-A34).
  • The table is the chart's text alternative.
  • No data for the filters: Clear filters.
