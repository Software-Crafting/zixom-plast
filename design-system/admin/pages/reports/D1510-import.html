    What it is

    Five step import wizard. Tally invoices is chosen; pick another type to see the Excel flow.

    What you can do

    • Tally invoices: upload the Sales Register export, map columns, review matches, import.
    • Match a customer whose GSTIN is not on file.
    • Other types: download the template, choose duplicate handling, download the error file.

    What happens next

    Imported invoices show in the app with Pay and in Invoices (D901). Undo from Import history (D1511).

    Edge cases and spec

    • Tally: customer by GSTIN, linked record by the reference in the narration, duplicates skipped by invoice number.
    • Automatic sync from TallyPrime is coming later (D1620).
    • Open with the client: TallyPrime or Tally ERP 9; who uploads the export each day.
    • Wrong file refused (EC-A29); row errors (EC-A31); duplicates (EC-A32); large files in the background (EC-A33).
