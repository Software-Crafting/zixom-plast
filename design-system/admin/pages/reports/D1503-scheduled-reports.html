What it is

Reports emailed on a schedule.

What you can do

  • Add a schedule for any report.
  • Pause, resume, send now, edit or delete from the row menu.
  • Retry a failed send.

What happens next

The email carries the report as PDF or Excel. Reports (D1501), Report view (D1502).

Edge cases and spec

  • Failed emails show a red banner and a Failed tag with the reason.
  • Paused schedules keep their settings.
  • Emails come from a Zixom address (EC-A27).
