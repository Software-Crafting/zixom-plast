What it is

Background exports of large lists, with their status and download link.

What you can do

  • Download a ready file.
  • Retry a failed export.
  • Copy the link or delete the export.

What happens next

A notification arrives when an export is ready. Started from Excel export (D1521).

Edge cases and spec

  • Over 5,000 rows runs in the background (EC-A34).
  • Links expire after 7 days; export again.
  • Failed exports can be retried. Every export is in the activity log.
