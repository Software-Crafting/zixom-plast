What it is

Notes engineers leave for the next engineer on a machine: tried, remains, site tips.

What you can do

  • Pin a note to the machine so every later job shows it.
  • Hide a wrong or old note.
  • Reply to the engineer.

What happens next

Visible notes show in the briefing of the next job on that machine (E216).

Edge cases and spec

  • Never visible to the customer.
  • Hidden notes stay in the log.
  • A note on an old ticket keeps its number even when the ticket is archived.
