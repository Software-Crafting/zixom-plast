    Delivery log

    What it is

    A push message to one engineer, a region or all engineers.

    What you can do

    • Choose who gets it, write a title and text.
    • Send now or schedule.
    • Open a sent message to see delivery; cancel a scheduled one.

    What happens next

    The message shows in the app inbox (E801). Delivery and failures are in the Delivery log (D1320).

    Edge cases and spec

    • Push fails: marked Failed in the log, email backup where allowed (EC-A26).
    • Schedule in the past is blocked (EC-A65).
    • Engineer assignments and messages ignore quiet hours (section 14).
