What it is

Certificates per engineer with issue and expiry dates and the file.

What you can do

  • Add a certificate, renew one, download the file.
  • Use the views for expiring and expired.

What happens next

The engineer sees them read only in the app (E808). Expiring certificates show on the engineer's page (D702).

Edge cases and spec

  • Expiring in 30 days is amber; expired is red.
  • Renewal keeps the old certificate in the history.
  • Files up to 10 MB (EC-A29 on failure).
