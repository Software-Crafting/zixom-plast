What it is

Known hazards per machine type or per site, with what to do.

What you can do

  • Add a hazard for a machine type or a site.
  • Click a row to edit; remove from the row menu or the drawer.
  • Export to Excel or CSV.

What happens next

Engineers see these in the job (E202) and the hazard notes (E905) and acknowledge them before the safety checklist.

Edge cases and spec

  • No hazards for a machine: the app shows standard safety rules.
  • Removing keeps the notes already shown on past jobs.
  • Incidents can add a hazard from their review (D731).
