What it is

Incidents and near misses engineers report from the app (E903).

What you can do

  • Click a row to review: add a review note and the action taken, then close.
  • Add an incident reported by phone.
  • Export the list to Excel or CSV.

What happens next

Hazard register (D732), Safety reports (D736), SOS (D730).

Edge cases and spec

  • Severity High goes to the work queue until closed.
  • The engineer cannot edit a sent report; Admin notes are separate.
  • Injury yes shows in red. A serious incident also sends banner, email and SMS (section 12).
