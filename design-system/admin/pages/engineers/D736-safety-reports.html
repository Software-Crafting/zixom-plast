    What it is

    Monthly safety picture: incidents, near misses and checklist failures from the engineer app.

    What you can do

    • Change the month; switch the chart to a table.
    • Filter by engineer, region, customer, machine type.
    • Export to Excel or CSV, or print.

    What happens next

    Incidents open D731; failed checks lead to the templates (D735); SOS history in D730.

    Edge cases and spec

    • Monthly view by default, IST months.
    • A checklist failure is an item answered No before work; the action taken is shown.
    • Export over 5,000 rows runs in the background (D1520).
