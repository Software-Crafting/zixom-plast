Status

What it is

Regions Zixom serves, their pincodes and the default engineer for each.

What you can do

  • Add a region or a pincode; import many from Excel.
  • Mark a pincode not serviceable, one or many.
  • Change the default engineer of a region.

What happens next

The app checks the site pincode on a request; Assign engineer (D404) suggests the default engineer first.

Edge cases and spec

  • Pincode not serviceable: the ticket shows a flag; Admin may still assign or call the customer (EC-A48).
  • Import with bad rows lists row numbers and reasons (EC-A31).
  • A pincode belongs to one region only.
