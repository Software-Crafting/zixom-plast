      What it is

      An engineer's SOS from the app (E904), with location, ticket and customer.

      What you can do

      • Call the engineer or the customer.
      • Acknowledge to stop the sound and the banner.
      • Add notes, share location, mark resolved with a reason.

      What happens next

      Incident report from the engineer (D731), Safety reports (D736).

      Edge cases and spec

      • Banner on every page until acknowledged (EC-A51). Active: sound and the tab title flashes.
      • Not acknowledged in 5 minutes: SMS to the owner's mobile.
      • Two at once: both listed. No signal: last location and time.
      • False alarm: resolved as false alarm, still logged. Offline shows last known data.
