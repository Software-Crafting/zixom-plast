What it is

Form to add a field engineer and send the app invitation.

What you can do

  • Enter name, mobile, code, region, designation, experience, skills, hours and photo.
  • Save and invite sends an SMS with the app link.

What happens next

The engineer signs in once (A17) on one phone and appears in suggestions. Opens Engineer detail (D702).

Edge cases and spec

  • Mobile already used by a customer is blocked with a link to D207 (EC-A35).
  • Save disables on first click (EC-A09); data kept if offline (EC-A01).
  • Invitation valid 7 days (section 14).
