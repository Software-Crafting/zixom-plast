    What it is

    Which sites need a safety induction and which engineers have a valid one.

    What you can do

    • Mark a site as needing an induction (switch on the right).
    • Mark an induction done and upload the certificate or pass.
    • Remind engineers or export the list.

    What happens next

    The engineer confirms an induction on site in the app (E906); it appears here. Site rules: D733.

    Edge cases and spec

    • Expired or missing induction: check in shows a warning; it does not block the job.
    • Ending within 60 days shows amber.
    • Turning the requirement off keeps past records.
