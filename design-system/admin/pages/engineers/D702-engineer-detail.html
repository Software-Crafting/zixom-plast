    What it is

    One engineer: profile, schedule, jobs, performance, leave, skills, device and notes. Opens with ?e=sandeep or ?e=anita.

    What you can do

    • Message, edit, send a test push.
    • Reset the device when a phone is lost or replaced.
    • Disable: open jobs are listed for reassignment first.

    What happens next

    Edit (D704), Message (D716), Assign engineer (D404) for each open job, Leave requests (D705).

    Edge cases and spec

    • One device per engineer; a reset allows a new device after one sign in.
    • Disabled: signed out and removed from suggestions (F10).
    • Location shows only during travel and at site (Q17).
