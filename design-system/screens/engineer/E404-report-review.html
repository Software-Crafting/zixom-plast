    What it is

    Final check of the visit report before it goes to Zixom and the customer.

    What you can do

    • Read every section and jump back with Edit.
    • Preview the PDF, get or redo the customer signature.
    • Submit the report.

    What happens next

    • Online: Report submitted, then Complete job (E210), Share PDF or the report (E405).
    • Offline: saved with Waiting to sync and sent later.

    Edge cases and spec

    • Missing items block Submit, each links to its fix (EC-58).
    • Submit fails: stays a draft with Retry. Server rejects a field: stays queued with the reason.
    • Photos still uploading: submit anyway. Cancelled job: kept as a draft.
    • Spec: section E4, E404 · EC-55, EC-58
