What it is

The engineer reports an injury, near miss, damage or unsafe condition on site.

What you can do

  • Choose what happened, when and where, and describe it.
  • Say whether anyone was injured and what was done at once.
  • Add up to 5 photos, then submit.

What happens next

  • The service manager gets the report with an INC number; it cannot be edited after submit.
  • Injury Yes shows Call 112 and Call manager and tells the manager immediately.
  • Offline it is saved on the phone, Waiting to sync.

Edge cases and spec

  • Missing fields list in an error summary on submit.
  • Failed photo upload keeps Retry and Remove.
  • Spec: section E9, E903.
