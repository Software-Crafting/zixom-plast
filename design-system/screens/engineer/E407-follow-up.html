What it is

Asks Zixom for a follow up visit on the same machine and customer, from the visit report.

What you can do

  • Pick a reason and describe it (required).
  • Suggest a date and time slot.
  • List the parts the visit needs.

What happens next

  • Send creates a follow up ticket for Zixom to schedule; the customer is notified.
  • Back to report returns to E401.

Edge cases and spec

  • A second open follow up for the machine is blocked, with a link to it.
  • Offline: saved with Waiting to sync and sent later.
  • Spec: section E4, E407
