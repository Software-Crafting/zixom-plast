What it is

The working screen during a visit, with a running timer from the saved check in time.

What you can do

  • Add photos, notes and voice notes; request a part; put on hold.
  • Call the customer, open machine history and hazard notes.
  • Log extra work the customer asks for (E221).

What happens next

  • Open report: the visit report (E401).
  • Complete: final checks (E210).

Edge cases and spec

  • App killed or phone restarted: back here with the right time.
  • Battery under 15%: save the report. Storage low: E1014.
  • Offline: everything is saved on the phone. Spec: section 10 and module E2, E207.
