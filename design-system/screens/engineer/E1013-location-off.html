What it is

Sheet shown when location services are off for the whole phone, at Start travel and Check in only.

What you can do

  • Open the phone's settings to turn Location on.
  • Continue without location.

What happens next

  • Without location the customer gets no live ETA (E205).
  • Check in still works but asks for a reason (E206).
  • The job keeps a Location is off banner until it is on.

Edge cases and spec

  • Permission denied for the app only: S07.
  • Spec: section E10, E1013 · EC-26.
