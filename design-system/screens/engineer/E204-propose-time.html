What it is

The engineer asks the customer for a different visit day and time window.

What you can do

  • Pick a day and a time window; past windows and Sundays are disabled.
  • Choose a reason and add an optional message.
  • Send to customer.

What happens next

  • The job keeps its current time until the customer agrees.
  • Accept or decline reaches the engineer as a notification; a decline asks for another time.

Edge cases and spec

  • Missing window or reason: error summary.
  • Offline: saved and sent later with Waiting to sync.
  • Spec: section 10 and module E2, E204.
