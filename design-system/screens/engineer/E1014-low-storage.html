What it is

Warning when the phone is low on space, with what the app can clear.

What you can do

  • See the space left.
  • Free uploaded photos and videos or old manuals.
  • Open the phone's storage settings.

What happens next

  • Once space is available the warning clears itself and the report can start (E401).
  • Only items already sent to Zixom are cleared; anything Waiting to sync stays.

Edge cases and spec

  • Under 200 MB a new report cannot start.
  • Phone storage full: S10.
  • Spec: section E10, E1014.
