What it is

In app camera for photos on the job in progress.

What you can do

  • Capture with flash, grid and multi shot.
  • Tag each photo: Before, After, Fault, Part, Nameplate or Other.
  • Retake, use or annotate a single shot; Done when finished.

What happens next

  • Each photo is saved on the phone first and queued for upload (E1008).
  • Annotate opens E1002; Done returns to the job.
  • Offline photos wait in the queue, Waiting to sync.

Edge cases and spec

  • Camera denied: S07, gallery still works.
  • Storage low: S10. 10 photos per job.
  • Spec: section E10, E1001.
