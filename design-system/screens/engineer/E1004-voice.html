What it is

Dictation sheet to fill findings or notes in the visit report by speaking.

What you can do

  • Start, speak, then Stop.
  • Edit the text or speak more.
  • Insert puts the text in the field.

What happens next

  • The text lands in the report field (E401), saved on the phone as a draft.

Edge cases and spec

  • Noisy plant: "Could not understand, try again".
  • Speech service offline: type instead.
  • Microphone denied: S07. Spec: section E10, E1004.
