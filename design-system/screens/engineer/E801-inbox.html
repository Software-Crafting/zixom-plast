What it is

The engineer's inbox: assignments, customer replies, parts, manager messages and leave decisions.

What you can do

  • Open a message to go to its job, request or leave
  • Answer a new assignment from the pinned time sensitive card
  • Mark all read

What happens next

  • New job opens E203; replies open E202; parts open E502
  • Reassigned or cancelled opens E215

Edge cases and spec

  • A removed record says so instead of opening (EC-61)
  • Many at once are grouped, one push per event (EC-62)
  • Offline shows the saved inbox
