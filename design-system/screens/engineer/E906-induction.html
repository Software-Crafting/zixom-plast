What it is

Records the site's safety induction, for sites that need one, before the safety checklist.

What you can do

  • Set the date and time of the induction.
  • Enter who briefed you.
  • Add an optional photo of the pass, then confirm.

What happens next

  • Confirm saves the induction on the job and leads to the E901 safety checklist.
  • Offline it is saved on the phone, Waiting to sync.

Edge cases and spec

  • Site does not need one: skipped, straight to E901.
  • Briefed by missing: error on confirm. Failed photo keeps Retry.
  • Spec: section E9, E906.
