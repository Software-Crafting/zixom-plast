What it is

Full screen signature pad, shown turned to landscape. The customer confirms the work on the engineer's phone.

What you can do

  • Draw a signature, enter name (required) and designation.
  • Clear the pad or save the signature.
  • Customer cannot sign: send an OTP to a site contact, or mark as refused with a reason.

What happens next

  • Save returns to E404; signature is stored with name and time.
  • The report shows Signed, Confirmed by OTP, Pending confirmation or Refused.

Edge cases and spec

  • Signature too small: "Please sign inside the box". Name 2 to 60 characters.
  • OTP not entered in 24 hours: submitted unconfirmed, manager told.
  • Offline: signature saved with the report.
  • Spec: section E4, E403 · EC-59, EC-60
