What it is

Opens from the push when Zixom cancels a job or moves it to another engineer: the reason and what to do next.

What you can do

  • Read the reason and when Zixom changed it.
  • Open the next job.
  • On site: call the service manager.
  • Acknowledge.

What happens next

  • Acknowledge removes the job from Today and Jobs.
  • Travel already started stays in the log.
  • Offline: the acknowledgement waits to sync.

Edge cases and spec

  • Cancelled while travelling or after check in each get their own message.
  • Spec: E2, E215 · EC-50 · flow: cancelled while travelling.
