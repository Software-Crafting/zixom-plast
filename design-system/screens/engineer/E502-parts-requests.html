Request parts

What it is

Every parts request the engineer sent, with the status from Stores. No prices.

What you can do

  • Switch between Active and Closed
  • Open a request to see its progress and courier
  • Confirm received for issued or dispatched parts
  • Request again after a rejection

What happens next

  • Stores is told when a part is confirmed received
  • Each status change sends a push (Parts request update)
  • New request opens E501

Edge cases and spec

  • Rejected shows the reason on the card
  • Offline: saved list; Confirm received waits to sync
  • Spec: section E5, E502 · Notifications: Parts request update
