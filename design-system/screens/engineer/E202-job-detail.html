What it is

Everything an engineer needs for one assigned job: customer, site, machine, issue and schedule.

What you can do

  • Call or WhatsApp the site contact, open the address in Maps.
  • Open machine history, hazard notes, briefing, parts, notes, timeline and attachments.
  • Use the action bar: it shows only the steps allowed for the current status.

What happens next

  • Offer: Accept or Decline (E203). Scheduled: Start travel (E205).
  • On the way: Check in (E206). In progress: report, hold, parts, escalate, complete.

Edge cases and spec

  • Not your job: no access. Cancelled while open: E215.
  • Photo fails: retry per item. Phone cannot call: copy number. Offline: saved job.
  • Pick a job above the frame for each status. Spec: section 10 and module E2, E202.
