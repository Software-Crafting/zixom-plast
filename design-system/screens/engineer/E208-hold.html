Put on hold

What it is

Pauses a job with a reason. The ticket stays assigned to the engineer.

What you can do

  • Pick a reason; parts and customer waits need a resume date, Other needs a note (10 to 300 characters).
  • Add an optional photo.
  • Request parts (E501) or report a safety incident (E903).

What happens next

  • Status becomes On hold; the customer and the manager see the reason.
  • Back on the job (E202), Resume continues the work.

Edge cases and spec

  • Report not finished: it stays as a draft.
  • Resume date in the past: error.
  • Offline: queued with Waiting to sync. Spec: section 10 and module E2, E208.
