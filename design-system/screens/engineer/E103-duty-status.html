What it is

Sheet over Today where the engineer sets their duty status.

What you can do

  • Pick Available, On job, On break or Off duty.
  • Add an optional reason, then Save.

What happens next

  • Save returns to E101 with the new pill; Zixom sees the status.
  • On break and Off duty hold new offers.

Edge cases and spec

  • Off duty is refused while a job is in progress.
  • Offline: saves on the phone and syncs later (E102).
  • Spec: E1, E103
