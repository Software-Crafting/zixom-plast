What it is

The engineer's start screen: what is next, what is waiting, what is unsent.

What you can do

  • Change duty status from the pill (E103).
  • Accept or decline an offer before its timer runs out.
  • Navigate, call the site contact or start travel on the next job.
  • Open any of today's jobs.

What happens next

  • Accept or Decline opens E203; Start travel opens E205.
  • The sync chip opens E102.
  • After 5 PM the End of day summary link appears (E1009).

Edge cases and spec

  • Two offers show their own timers; an overlap is amber on the later job.
  • Job reassigned while open: card updates and a toast says why.
  • Offline: saved jobs stay usable; nothing saved asks to connect once.
  • Spec: E1, E101 · EC-04, EC-55
