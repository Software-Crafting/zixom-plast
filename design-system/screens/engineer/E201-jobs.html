What it is

Every job assigned to the engineer, in tabs New, Upcoming, In progress, Completed and All.

What you can do

  • Switch tabs; New shows how many need a response.
  • Search by ticket, customer or serial, also offline in saved jobs.
  • Filter by date, priority, coverage, service type and status; sort by time window, priority or newest.

What happens next

  • A new job opens E203 to accept or decline.
  • Any other job opens E202.
  • The cloud button opens the sync queue (E102).

Edge cases and spec

  • Timer turns red when nearly due; each tab has its own empty wording.
  • A job reassigned away leaves after it is acknowledged (E215).
  • Offline: saved jobs only; unsent changes show Waiting to sync.
  • Spec: E2, E201 · EC-55
