What it is

Every report on this phone that has not reached Zixom: failed, waiting to sync and drafts.

What you can do

  • Open a draft on the section where it stopped.
  • Retry or review a failed item.
  • Delete a draft after confirming.

What happens next

  • Open goes to E401; Review opens the conflict screen E1007.
  • Waiting items send by themselves when online.

Edge cases and spec

  • Empty: "No drafts". Drafts older than 30 days are flagged.
  • Submitted reports cannot be deleted here.
  • Spec: section E4, E406 · EC-56, EC-57
