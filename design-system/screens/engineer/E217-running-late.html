What it is

Tells the customer the engineer will be late, from Job detail while on the way.

What you can do

  • Pick a reason: traffic, previous job, vehicle problem or other
  • Pick +15 min to +2 h or type a new arrival time
  • See the exact message the customer gets

What happens next

  • Send update pushes the reason and new time to the customer (C516)
  • Offline, the update waits to sync and sends when back online
  • Back to job returns to E202

Edge cases and spec

  • Up to 3 updates per job; after that, call the customer
  • Over 2 hours late also alerts the service manager
  • Spec: section E11, E217 · EC-52
