What it is

The Machines tab: every machine the company owns with warranty, AMC and open requests.

What you can do

  • Search by model, serial or site
  • Filter and sort; remove filter chips
  • Long press a card for quick actions
  • Scan a machine QR to open it
  • Report a missing machine

What happens next

  • A card opens C202
  • Missing a machine? opens C204; only Zixom adds machines
  • Scan opens C205
  • AMC opens the AMC segment, C401

Edge cases and spec

  • Pages of 20; a failed page shows an inline Try again
  • Pending machines show a grey chip and a limited detail
  • Spec: section 8, C201 · C2 rules
