ZC-500

What it is

Preventive visits Zixom planned for every AMC contract, as a list or a calendar.

What you can do

  • Switch List or Calendar; filter by machine
  • Confirm a planned visit
  • Ask to reschedule, or ask for a new date after a miss
  • Open the report of a done visit

What happens next

  • Confirm marks the visit Confirmed for Zixom and the engineer
  • Reschedule opens C407
  • Open report opens the service report (C512)

Edge cases and spec

  • Statuses: Planned, Confirmed, Done, Missed with the reason
  • No visits yet: empty state
  • Reminders 7 days and 1 day before (push and email)
  • Spec: C406 · C4 rules
