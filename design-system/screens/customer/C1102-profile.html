Profile photo

What it is

The signed in person's own details. No role field: customer permissions are flat.

What you can do

  • Change or remove the photo.
  • Edit name, email and designation, then Save.
  • Change the mobile number (needs an OTP).
  • Verify a new email.

What happens next

  • Save shows a toast and goes back to More.
  • Change mobile opens A19; Verify now opens A21.

Edge cases and spec

  • Email already used by another account: inline error, Save off.
  • Photo upload failed: retry or remove the file.
  • Wrong or expired OTP is handled in A19 (EC-13).
  • Spec: section C11, C1102 · EC-13
