What it is

Asks Zixom to move a planned preventive visit to another date and time window.

What you can do

  • Pick a new date and window
  • Pick a reason, add an optional note
  • Send the request, or ask Zixom to propose a date

What happens next

  • Pending approval: the old date stays until Zixom approves
  • Approval or a new proposal arrives by push and email
  • View schedule returns to C406

Edge cases and spec

  • Inside 48 hours of the visit: call Zixom (shown as if opened then)
  • No free slots: Ask Zixom to propose
  • Offline disables Send
  • Spec: C407
