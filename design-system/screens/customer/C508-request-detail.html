What it is

One service ticket from request to closure: status, engineer, request, messages and actions.

What you can do

  • Open the machine or the engineer profile
  • Message Zixom or add information
  • Actions follow the status: cancel, reschedule, call, track, confirm, rate
  • Share or copy the request number

What happens next

  • Cancel opens C511, Reschedule C510
  • Estimate opens C509, report C512
  • A push while open shows the refresh banner

Edge cases and spec

  • Engineer changed, visit moved by Zixom, cancelled by Zixom (EC-50)
  • Not found S05, no access S06
  • Engineer phone hidden after closure; 12 photos as a gallery
  • Open with ?ticket=TKT-2026-0123; Spec: C508 detail
