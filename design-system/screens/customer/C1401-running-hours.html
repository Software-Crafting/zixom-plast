Entry

What it is

Hour meter log of one machine, newest first, for anyone in the customer company.

What you can do

  • See the current total and hours since the last service.
  • Open an entry for who entered it, the note and the meter photo.
  • Add a new reading.

What happens next

  • Add entry opens C1402.
  • Engineers see the latest reading on the job.

Edge cases and spec

  • No readings: empty state with Add entry.
  • A reading lower than the one before is refused in C1402.
  • Wrong machine id: S05 (EC-20).
  • Spec: section C14, C1401 · EC-02, EC-20
