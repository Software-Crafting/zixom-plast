    What it is

    Status of one dispute: amount, reason, Zixom's reply and the timeline.

    What you can do

    • Under review: add a message and files.
    • Resolved: open the credit note and follow the refund.
    • Rejected: read Zixom's reason and contact support.

    What happens next

    • Open credit note goes to C1603; the refund returns to the account paid from.
    • Contact support opens C605.

    Edge cases and spec

    • Unknown dispute: S05 (EC-20).
    • Status model: Submitted, Under review, Resolved or Rejected.
    • Spec: section C16, C1602 · EC-20
