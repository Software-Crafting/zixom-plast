What it is

Step 3 of the warranty claim wizard: proof of the fault.

What you can do

  • Add fault photos or a video (at least 1, up to 10 photos and 1 video of 60 seconds).
  • Add the nameplate photo (required).
  • Add up to 3 other documents (PDF, JPG, PNG).
  • Pause, retry or remove each file.

What happens next

  • Next opens C306 once the minimum is met.
  • Failed files can be left out after a confirmation.

Edge cases and spec

  • Camera denied (S07), too large or wrong type (EC-29, EC-30).
  • Long video trimmed; large video on mobile data asks first.
  • Uploads resume after the app was closed.
  • Spec: section C3, C305
