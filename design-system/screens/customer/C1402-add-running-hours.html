What it is

Form to add an hour meter reading for one machine.

What you can do

  • Enter the reading and pick the date (no future dates).
  • Add an optional note and a meter photo.
  • Save, also offline.

What happens next

  • Save returns to C1401 with the new entry.
  • A second reading on the same day asks to replace the first.

Edge cases and spec

  • Lower than the last reading: inline error, Save off.
  • Photo over 10 MB or failed upload: per file Remove or Retry (EC-29, EC-31).
  • Offline: saved and sent when back online (EC-03).
  • Spec: section C14, C1402 · EC-29, EC-31, EC-40
