What it is

Optional feedback when the customer decides not to renew an AMC contract.

What you can do

  • Pick a reason and add a comment
  • Submit, or Skip without a reason
  • Change your mind and renew

What happens next

  • The contract stays active until its end date
  • Renewal reminders stop; Zixom sees the reason
  • View contract returns to C402

Edge cases and spec

  • Reason and comment are optional (skippable)
  • Offline disables Submit
  • Spec: C408 · AMC reminders
