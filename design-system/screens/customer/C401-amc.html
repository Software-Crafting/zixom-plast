What it is

The AMC segment of the Machines tab: every AMC contract of the company and the plans on offer.

What you can do

  • Switch Active, Expiring, Expired or Plans
  • Open a contract
  • Renew or pay from a contract card
  • Buy AMC from the floating button

What happens next

  • A card opens the contract (C402)
  • Renew opens C405; Pay now opens payment (C803)
  • Buy AMC opens the wizard (C404); Plans open C403

Edge cases and spec

  • Expiring soon is outlined with the days left
  • Expired keeps Renew for the 7 day grace period
  • No AMC: View plans; error has Try again
  • Spec: C4 AMC, C401 · AMC status model · F5
