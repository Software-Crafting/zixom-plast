ZC-500

What it is

The primary contact asks Zixom to hand the flag to another active member.

What you can do

  • Choose the new primary contact
  • Give a reason (required)
  • Send to Zixom

What happens next

  • The request shows as pending
  • Zixom confirms by notification; the new person then sees Team members

Edge cases and spec

  • Only the primary contact sees this; a pending request blocks a second
  • No other member: request a new user first
  • Spec: C18, C1114 · F11
