What it is

Appeal a rejected warranty claim, once, within 7 days of the decision.

What you can do

  • Read the rejection reason.
  • Explain why Zixom should look again (20 to 1000 characters).
  • Add up to 5 files of extra evidence.
  • Convert to a paid service request instead.

What happens next

  • Submit appeal sends it; the claim goes back to Under review in C308.
  • Convert opens C503 with the machine.

Edge cases and spec

  • Window expired or already appealed: only the paid service request is left.
  • Offline: Submit disabled.
  • Spec: section C3, C310 · business rules (appeal once in 7 days)
