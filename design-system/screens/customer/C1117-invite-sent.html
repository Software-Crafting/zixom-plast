What it is

Confirmation after the primary contact invites a colleague in C1105.

What you can do

  • Share the invite link by WhatsApp or any app
  • Retry a failed share
  • Done returns to Team members

What happens next

  • The colleague signs in with the invited number and joins the company (A18)
  • They show as Invited in Team members until then

Edge cases and spec

  • Link valid 7 days; Resend makes a new one
  • Share failed shows Try again
