What it is

Everyone in the company with masked mobile and status. Only the primary contact sees it.

What you can do

  • Invite a person by name and mobile.
  • Resend, share or revoke an invite.
  • Disable, enable or remove a member.
  • Change the primary contact.

What happens next

  • Send invite opens C1117; they get an SMS link that expires in 7 days.
  • A disabled person is signed out on their next request (A16).
  • Change primary contact opens C1114.

Edge cases and spec

  • Number in another company or of an engineer: blocked, no invite.
  • The primary contact can't be disabled.
  • 25 people at most; Invite is off at the limit.
  • Spec: section C11, C1105 · EC-18, EC-19
