What it is

Everyone in the company with masked mobile and status, plus pending requests. Only the primary contact sees it.

What you can do

  • Request a new user: name, mobile, designation, optional email.
  • Cancel a pending request.
  • Disable, enable or remove a member.
  • Change the primary contact.

What happens next

  • Send request opens C1117; it goes to Zixom's work queue and shows as Requested here.
  • Zixom adds the person and sends app access by SMS (A18).
  • A disabled person is signed out on their next request (A16).
  • Change primary contact opens C1114.

Edge cases and spec

  • Only Zixom adds people; nobody joins without Zixom.
  • Number already on the team: blocked. Zixom checks other companies and engineers.
  • The primary contact can't be disabled.
  • 25 people at most; requests are off at the limit.
  • Spec: section C11, C1105 · EC-18, EC-19
