What it is

The customer home tab. Shows what needs attention and the main actions for the selected company.

What you can do

  • Open an alert or View all alerts
  • Tap a summary card to open that list
  • Use a quick action or Machine down?

What happens next

  • Cards open C201, C502, C301 and C801
