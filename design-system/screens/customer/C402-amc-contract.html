    What it is

    One AMC contract: plan, machines, what is included, visits, invoices and the contract PDF.

    What you can do

    • Use the main button: Raise breakdown request, Renew or Pay now
    • Open a machine, the visit schedule or an invoice
    • Download the contract PDF
    • More: reschedule a visit, ask a question, do not renew

    What happens next

    • Raise opens C503 with the machine picked
    • Renew opens C405; Pay now opens C803
    • Visits open C406; invoices C802; PDF C902

    Edge cases and spec

    • Suspended: red banner with the amount and Pay now
    • Expired: renew within 7 days, then Buy AMC
    • All visits used: chargeable option (EC-48); removed machine shows the date
