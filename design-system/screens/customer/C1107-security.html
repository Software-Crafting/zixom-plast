Sign out other devices?

What it is

App lock and the devices signed in to this account. A customer can use 2 devices.

What you can do

  • Turn Face ID app lock on or off.
  • Sign out the other device.
  • Sign out of this device.

What happens next

  • A signed out device needs an OTP to sign in again.
  • Signing out this device asks first, then opens A03.

Edge cases and spec

  • Face ID not set up: Set up opens S19.
  • Biometrics changed on the phone: app lock turns off (EC-39).
  • Third device signs in: A23 (EC-12).
  • Spec: section C11, C1107 · EC-12, EC-39
