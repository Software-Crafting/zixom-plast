What it is

Sheet from More on Machine detail that shares a machine card with colleagues or a service provider.

What you can do

  • Preview the card: model, serial, warranty, AMC, site.
  • Pick image or PDF.
  • Share, or copy the details as text.

What happens next

  • Share opens the phone share sheet.
  • Copy puts the details on the clipboard.

Edge cases and spec

  • Offline with saved data: shares the saved card.
  • Offline and not saved: Share off.
  • Share fails: S17.
  • Spec: section C14, C1412 · EC-02
