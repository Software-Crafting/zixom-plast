What it is

Renews an AMC contract: same or another plan, add or remove machines, pick the term.

What you can do

  • Untick or add machines
  • Change the plan and the 1, 2 or 3 year term
  • Pay now, Request quote or Do not renew

What happens next

  • Pay opens C803; the new term starts the day after the end
  • Request quote: confirmation, quote by notification and email
  • Do not renew opens C408

Edge cases and spec

  • Window opens 60 days before the end; earlier shows the opening date
  • Up to 7 days after expiry: same contract, no gap; after that Buy AMC
  • Price increase is noted against last year's price
  • Spec: C405 · AMC reminders 60, 30, 7, 0 days
