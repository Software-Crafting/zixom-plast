What it is

Step 5 of the warranty claim wizard: check everything and submit.

What you can do

  • Edit any step and come back here.
  • Tick the declaration (used as intended, no outside repair).
  • Submit the claim.

What happens next

  • Success shows the claim number and review within 2 working days.
  • View claim opens C308; the claim can no longer be edited.

Edge cases and spec

  • Warranty ended meanwhile: Submit blocked, convert to paid service (EC-47).
  • Network dropped: not sure it was sent (EC-03). Duplicate: link to the claim (EC-41).
  • Offline: blocked, draft kept. Failed upload: Retry.
  • Spec: section C3, C307 · EC-03, EC-41, EC-47
