What it is

The company's sites with address, site contact and how many machines each has.

What you can do

  • Add a site.
  • Edit name, pincode, address, map pin, contact and access notes.
  • Open a site in Maps.
  • Delete a site with no machines.

What happens next

  • Add site opens C1507.
  • Save shows a toast and updates the card.
  • A blocked delete offers View machines (C201).

Edge cases and spec

  • Site with machines can't be deleted.
  • Pincode lookup failed: city and state are typed in (EC-45).
  • No sites yet: empty state with Add site.
  • Spec: section C11, C1104 · EC-45
