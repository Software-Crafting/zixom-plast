What it is

Tells Zixom a machine is missing from the account. Customers never add machines themselves.

What you can do

  • Give the serial or a nameplate photo (one is enough)
  • Add the model if known and pick the site
  • See your reports and their status

What happens next

  • The report goes to Zixom's work queue and shows as Requested
  • Zixom adds the machine, usually within 1 working day; it then appears in C201

Edge cases and spec

  • Neither serial nor photo: error on send
  • Photo too large or wrong type (EC-29, EC-30); offline keeps the form (EC-03)
  • Client decision 26 Nov 2026 · Spec: C2, C204
