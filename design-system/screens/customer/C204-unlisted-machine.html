What it is

Reports a second hand or older machine Zixom cannot find, for verification.

What you can do

  • Enter model, serial, year, site and contact
  • Add a nameplate photo and an optional invoice
  • Retry or remove a failed upload
  • Resubmit after a rejection

What happens next

  • Submit adds the machine to C201 as Pending verification
  • Zixom notifies when it is linked or rejected

Edge cases and spec

  • Too large or wrong type (EC-29, EC-30)
  • Upload fails: retry or send without it (EC-31)
  • Offline keeps the form (EC-03)
  • Spec: C2, C204
