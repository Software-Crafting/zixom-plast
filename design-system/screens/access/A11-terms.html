1. Who can use the app

Zixom Connect is for people who work for a company that owns Zixom machines, and for Zixom service engineers. Your company's primary contact decides who joins your company.

2. Your account

You sign in with your mobile number and a one time code. Keep your phone safe. You can be signed in on up to 2 phones.

3. Service, warranty and AMC

Visit times are confirmed by Zixom. Warranty claims are decided by the service manager within 2 working days. AMC covers labour unless your plan says otherwise.

4. Payments

Payments go through Razorpay in Zixom Plast's name. Full payment only. The app never stores card details.

Version 1.1, 21 Nov 2026. Template drafted for Zixom to review.

What we keep

Your name, mobile number, email, designation and the company you work for. Records of machines, service visits, claims, orders and payments.

Photos and location

We remove location data from photos you upload. We read an engineer's location only while travelling to you and at check in.

How long we keep it

Photos, reports and messages stay for the life of the machine record. Business records are kept as the law requires, even after you delete your account.
